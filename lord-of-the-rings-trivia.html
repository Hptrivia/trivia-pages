Lord of The Rings Trivia Quiz

The Lord of the Rings follows the hobbit Frodo Baggins, who inherits a powerful One Ring and must carry it across Middle-earth to destroy it in the fires of Mount Doom before the Dark Lord Sauron reclaims it. He sets out with a Fellowship that includes the wizard Gandalf, the ranger Aragorn, Legolas, Gimli, and his loyal friend Sam.

Q1. Who carries the One Ring to Mount Doom?

Answer: Frodo Baggins. Frodo inherits the Ring from his uncle Bilbo and takes it on the quest to destroy it.

Q2. What is the name of Frodo's loyal gardener and friend?

Answer: Samwise Gamgee. Sam follows Frodo all the way to Mordor and helps carry him up Mount Doom.

Q3. Which wizard guides the Fellowship of the Ring?

Answer: Gandalf. Gandalf the Grey leads the Fellowship until he falls in Moria.

Q4. Which creature was once called Sméagol?

Answer: Gollum. The Ring corrupted Sméagol over centuries into the twisted creature called Gollum.

Q5. What is the name of the land where the hobbits live?

Answer: The Shire. The Shire is a peaceful countryside home to the hobbits, Frodo, Sam, Merry and Pippin.

Q6. What is the name of Frodo's sword, which glows blue near orcs?

Answer: Sting. Bilbo gives the elven blade Sting to Frodo, and it glows blue when orcs are close.

Q7. What race is Legolas?

Answer: Elf. Legolas is a Sindar Elf and a prince of Mirkwood, known for his archery.

Q8. What is the name of Aragorn's reforged sword?

Answer: Andúril. Andúril was reforged from the shards of Narsil, the sword that cut the Ring from Sauron's hand.

Q9. In which fiery mountain was the One Ring forged and where must it be destroyed?

Answer: Mount Doom. Mount Doom, also called Orodruin, is where Sauron forged the Ring.

Q10. Who is the king of Rohan in The Two Towers?

Answer: Théoden. Théoden is freed from Saruman's influence and leads Rohan at Helm's Deep.

Q11. Who is the Steward of Gondor and the father of Boromir and Faramir?

Answer: Denethor. Denethor rules Gondor as its Steward while the throne is empty.

Q12. What is the name of the pony that carries the Fellowship's supplies until Moria?

Answer: Bill. Sam takes care of Bill the pony, who must be left behind at the mines of Moria.

Q13. What is the name of the giant spider Frodo and Sam face at Cirith Ungol?

Answer: Shelob. Shelob is an ancient spider who lives in a tunnel above Mordor.

Q14. Which forest is home to Treebeard the Ent?

Answer: Fangorn. Fangorn Forest is where Merry and Pippin meet Treebeard.

Q15. Which member of the Fellowship dies defending Merry and Pippin from the Uruk-hai?

Answer: Boromir. Boromir falls while trying to protect the hobbits, repenting for his attempt to take the Ring.

Q16. How many Rings of Power were given to the Dwarf-lords?

Answer: Seven. Seven Rings went to the Dwarves, nine to Men, and three to the Elves.

Q17. The dragon Smaug guards the treasure of which mountain?

Answer: Erebor. Smaug takes over the Lonely Mountain, Erebor, in The Hobbit.

Q18. What are the two main Elvish languages that Tolkien invented?

Answer: Quenya and Sindarin. Tolkien was a philologist who invented languages before creating stories for them.

Q19. In which year was The Fellowship of the Ring first published?

Answer: 1954. Tolkien published The Fellowship of the Ring in 1954, and The Hobbit had come out in 1937.

Q20. Which Nazgûl leader is known as the Witch-king of what?

Answer: Angmar. The Witch-king of Angmar leads the Ringwraiths and is killed by Éowyn.

About the Lord of The Rings quiz

The Lord of the Rings is the deep end of fantasy trivia. Tolkien wrote languages, histories, and genealogies that stretch back thousands of years before the Ring is even destroyed, and the films distilled that world into some of the most quotable cinema ever made. Casual fans remember the Fellowship, Mordor, and "one does not simply"; the truly devoted can name the members of the Fellowship in order, trace the Ring's journey, and explain who forged what and why. A strong quiz here sits comfortably between the movies most people love and the lore the hardcore have memorised.

Questions cover the trilogy — the members of the Fellowship, Gollum and the Ring, the realms of Gondor and Rohan, the battles of Helm's Deep and Minas Tirith, and the long journey to Mount Doom.

What this Lord of The Rings quiz covers

This quiz spans the journey to destroy the One Ring — the Fellowship and its members, the peoples of Middle-earth, the key locations from the Shire to Mount Doom, and the battles along the way. Expect questions on the characters everyone remembers alongside details of the wider world Tolkien built. The early questions cover the well-known story beats; the tougher ones reward people who know Middle-earth beyond a single viewing.

Lord of The Rings quiz — frequently asked questions

Is this about the films or the books?

It leans on the story most fans know from the films, treating the journey as one continuous tale. Where a widely known detail comes from the books, it may appear too, but no deep book-only knowledge is required to start.

Does it include The Hobbit?

The focus is the Lord of the Rings story itself. Some questions may touch shared characters or history, but you do not need The Hobbit to answer the core quiz.

How lore-heavy is it?

The early questions are approachable for anyone who has seen the films. The later ones reach into the deeper lore — names, places, and history — that reward dedicated fans.
