Stranger Things Trivia Quiz

Stranger Things is set in the small town of Hawkins, Indiana in the 1980s. When a boy named Will Byers disappears, his friends stumble onto a shadowy parallel dimension called the Upside Down — and a girl with psychic powers known only as Eleven, who escaped a secret government lab.

Q1. In which fictional town is Stranger Things set?

Answer: Hawkins, Indiana. Hawkins is a small Indiana town that hides a secret government laboratory.

Q2. Which breakfast food does Eleven love?

Answer: Eggo waffles. Eleven's love of Eggo waffles becomes a running gag from the first season.

Q3. Which tabletop game do Mike, Dustin, Lucas and Will play in the opening scene?

Answer: Dungeons & Dragons. The boys use the game's monsters to name the creatures they meet, including the Demogorgon.

Q4. Which young character disappears in the first episode?

Answer: Will Byers. Will vanishes after riding his bike home, which starts the search for him.

Q5. What is the name of the dark alternate dimension in the series?

Answer: The Upside Down. The Upside Down is a twisted copy of Hawkins covered in vines and spores.

Q6. Who is the chief of police in Hawkins?

Answer: Jim Hopper. Hopper is a gruff chief who becomes a guardian for Eleven.

Q7. What is the name of the monster in season one?

Answer: The Demogorgon. The Demogorgon comes through a gate from the Upside Down to hunt in Hawkins.

Q8. How does Joyce Byers communicate with Will in season one?

Answer: Through Christmas lights. Joyce hangs lights on her wall, and they blink to spell out messages from Will.

Q9. What is Eleven's real first name?

Answer: Jane. Eleven is known as Jane Hopper after Hopper adopts her.

Q10. Which ice cream shop does Steve Harrington work at in season three?

Answer: Scoops Ahoy. Steve and Robin work at Scoops Ahoy, the ice cream parlour in Starcourt Mall.

Q11. Which facility does Dr. Martin Brenner run?

Answer: Hawkins National Laboratory. Hawkins Lab is the secret facility where Eleven was raised and trained.

Q12. Which Kate Bush song saves Max in season four?

Answer: Running Up That Hill. Max plays her favourite track, and the song helps her to escape Vecna's grasp.

Q13. Who is the main villain of season four?

Answer: Vecna. Vecna hunts young people in Hawkins in the fourth season.

Q14. What is the name of the shopping mall that opens in season three?

Answer: Starcourt Mall. The Starcourt Mall hides a Russian base underneath it.

Q15. What is the name of Dustin's pet who turns out to be a Demodog?

Answer: Dart. Dustin finds Dart in a garbage can and feeds him candy, unaware he is a baby Demodog.

Q16. In which year does the first season of Stranger Things take place?

Answer: 1983. The story begins in November 1983 in a small Indiana town.

Q17. Who created Stranger Things?

Answer: The Duffer Brothers. Matt and Ross Duffer created the show, and released it on Netflix in 2016.

Q18. What is the name of Will's older brother?

Answer: Jonathan. Jonathan Byers loves photography and music, and helps to find Will.

Q19. Which actor plays Eleven?

Answer: Millie Bobby Brown. Millie Bobby Brown was cast as Eleven when she was only twelve years old.

Q20. Which actor plays Joyce Byers, Will's mother?

Answer: Winona Ryder. Winona Ryder plays Joyce, who will do anything to find her missing son.

About the Stranger Things quiz

Stranger Things wears its mysteries on its sleeve, which is what makes it so much fun to quiz. Every season layers in new names, new monsters, and a fresh set of eighties references, and fans love keeping the tangled timeline straight. Casual viewers remember Eleven, the Upside Down, and Demogorgons; devoted fans can trace who disappeared when, name the town and the lab, and recall the songs and running gags woven through each season. A good quiz here rewards both the big emotional moments and the small details the Duffer brothers hide in plain sight.

Questions cover every season — Eleven and her powers, the Upside Down, the Demogorgon and the Mind Flayer, the town of Hawkins, and the group of kids at the centre of it all.

What this Stranger Things quiz covers

Questions draw from across the seasons — the core group of kids and teens, Eleven and her powers, the Upside Down and its creatures, the town of Hawkins, and the mysteries that drive each year. Expect a spread from the moments everyone remembers to the finer details. The early questions cover the widely known story; the harder ones reward viewers who have watched the whole series closely.
