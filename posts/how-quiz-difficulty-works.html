Easy, Medium, Hard, Expert: How Difficulty Works in Our Quizzes

Every quiz on Trivia Gauntlet uses the same structure: 20 questions, split into four difficulty levels of five questions each. This article explains what each level means, and how to pick a quiz that suits how well you know a show.

Easy

Easy questions are about the things nearly every viewer remembers: the main characters, the setting, the famous catchphrases and the biggest plot points. If you have watched a series once, you should be able to answer most of them.

Medium

Medium questions ask for a little more attention. They cover supporting characters, jobs, relationships and events that a casual viewer might half remember. They are where a regular fan starts to pull ahead.

Hard

Hard questions go into details: smaller characters, particular scenes and the finer points of the plot. People who have rewatched a series will do well here.

Expert

The expert questions are for true fans. They can cover the cast, the creators, release dates and the more unusual details of the show. Even so, each one has a clear, checkable answer, and it comes with an explanation after you pick.

Choosing a Quiz

If you love a show but you are not sure how well you know it, start with The Office, Friends or Stranger Things, where the early questions are gentle. If you want a stronger test, try Breaking Bad, The Wire or Attack on Titan, where the hard and expert levels cover a lot of detail.

What Your Score Means

A score of 15 or more out of 20 means you know the show very well. Ten to fourteen is a solid result for a regular viewer, and anything lower just means there is more to rewatch. Every quiz can be replayed, and the explanations help you remember the answers next time.
