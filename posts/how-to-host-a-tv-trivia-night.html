How to Host a TV Trivia Night at Home

A trivia night does not need a pub, a quizmaster or a stack of printed sheets. With a few friends, a screen and a quiz on a topic everybody knows, you can run a fun evening in about an hour. Here is a simple way to do it using the quizzes on Trivia Gauntlet.

1. Pick a Show Everyone Knows

The best trivia nights use a show that most of the group has seen. A sitcom such as The Office or Friends works well because nearly everyone can answer a few questions. For a group of bigger fans, try Game of Thrones or Harry Potter, where the harder questions will separate the experts from the casual viewers.

2. Decide How to Play

There are two easy formats. In the first, everyone plays the same quiz on their own phone at the same time and compares scores at the end. In the second, one person reads the questions aloud from a shared screen, and teams write their answers down before anyone reveals the correct one.

The second format is better for groups, because the discussion is half the fun. Every quiz has 20 questions, so a single quiz makes a good round.

3. Use the Difficulty Levels

Every quiz on the site is built from five easy, five medium, five hard and five expert questions. You can use this to make the scoring fairer. For example, you could give one point for each easy question and two, three and four points for the higher levels. Newcomers can still score well on the early questions, while the experts have the harder ones to look forward to.

4. Keep Score and Settle Ties

Keep a simple scoreboard on paper. If two teams tie, play a sudden-death round from a second quiz on the same topic, or ask a single tiebreaker such as "how many seasons did the show run for?", where the closest answer wins.

5. Read the Explanations

Each quiz shows the correct answer with a short explanation, and the full answer key is printed under the quiz. When a question causes an argument, this is the place to settle it, and it often starts a good conversation about the show.

6. Try a Themed Evening

To make the night feel special, add snacks or drinks that fit the theme. Anime fans could try Naruto or One Piece, while a gaming night suits Minecraft or Pokémon. You can browse all the quizzes on the quiz home page.
