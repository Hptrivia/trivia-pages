How the Quizzes on Trivia Gauntlet Are Made

Trivia Gauntlet started from something simple: I like trivia, and I like rewatching shows. Some of my favourite series are ones I have gone back to four, five or even ten times, and that is a big part of why building a trivia site interested me in the first place.

This page explains how the quizzes here are put together, so you know what to expect when you play one.

What Every Quiz Contains

Each quiz has 20 multiple-choice questions with four options and one correct answer. The questions are split into four difficulty levels: five easy, five medium, five hard and five expert. The easy ones set the pace and the expert ones ask for real recall.

After every question, the quiz shows the correct answer with a short explanation, and the same answers and explanations are printed under the quiz. The point is that you should learn something even when you get a question wrong.

What Makes a Good Trivia Question?

A good trivia question is not only about finding an obscure fact. A lot of the difficulty comes from how the options are written. If two of the four choices are obviously fake, even a hard fact becomes easy. If the choices are close enough together, even a simple fact makes the player think.

I also try to avoid questions that can be argued either way. If a fact has more than one reasonable answer, or if I cannot confirm it, it does not go into a quiz. That means the quizzes lean toward main characters, big plot points and well-known details rather than trivia so obscure that nobody could check it.

How the Questions Are Written

I use AI tools to help draft questions and explanations, because writing hundreds of them from scratch by hand would take a long time. The draft is only the starting point. Questions are then read through, and any that are unclear, unfair or doubtful are rewritten or cut. Wrong answer options are adjusted so that they sound believable rather than silly.

Corrections

With this many questions, mistakes can slip through. If you think an answer is wrong, or you spot a badly worded question, please use the contact page and choose "Wrong Question / Answer". When a mistake is confirmed, the question is fixed or removed.

What Comes Next

The library is still small, with 27 quizzes so far, and it will grow one show at a time. If there is a series you want to see, you can request it on the contact page. You can browse everything on the quiz home page.
