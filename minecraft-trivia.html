Minecraft Trivia Quiz

Minecraft is a sandbox game where players gather resources and place blocks to build and survive in vast, procedurally generated worlds. By day you mine and craft tools; by night you fend off mobs like creepers, skeletons, and zombies, before eventually venturing into the Nether and the End to face the Ender Dragon.

Q1. Which mob hisses and explodes when it gets close to a player?

Answer: Creeper. Creepers are silent green mobs that explode, and they are easy to recognise from their hissing.

Q2. Which two materials are needed to craft a wooden pickaxe?

Answer: Planks and sticks. A wooden pickaxe is one of the first tools and needs three planks and two sticks.

Q3. What is the name of the fiery dimension reached through an obsidian portal?

Answer: The Nether. The Nether is full of lava, fortresses and dangerous mobs.

Q4. Which passive mob drops leather and raw beef?

Answer: Cow. Cows are a source of food and leather, and they can be milked with a bucket.

Q5. Which company bought Minecraft in 2014?

Answer: Microsoft. Microsoft bought Mojang and Minecraft for around 2.5 billion US dollars.

Q6. What is the final boss of Minecraft?

Answer: The Ender Dragon. The Ender Dragon lives in the End and is the main boss of the game.

Q7. Which Nether material is smelted to create netherite scrap?

Answer: Ancient debris. Ancient debris is rare and found deep in the Nether.

Q8. Which mob teleports and becomes hostile if you look it in the eyes?

Answer: Enderman. Endermen are tall and dark, and they are provoked by direct eye contact.

Q9. Which item do you use to tame a wolf?

Answer: Bones. Feeding bones to a wolf can tame it, and a tamed wolf becomes a loyal pet.

Q10. Which workstation block gives a villager the librarian profession?

Answer: Lectern. A villager without a job who finds a lectern becomes a librarian and trades enchanted books.

Q11. Which mob drops the Totem of Undying?

Answer: Evoker. Evokers appear in woodland mansions and raids, and they drop the totem, which can save a player from death.

Q12. In which biome do mooshrooms naturally spawn?

Answer: Mushroom Fields. Mooshrooms are red-and-white cows found only on the rare Mushroom Fields biome.

Q13. How many frames are there in a complete End portal?

Answer: Twelve. The portal is made of twelve frames, and each needs an Eye of Ender.

Q14. Which boss drops the Nether Star?

Answer: The Wither. The Wither is a boss the player builds, and it drops a Nether Star which is used to make a beacon.

Q15. Which block is needed to build a Nether portal?

Answer: Obsidian. A Nether portal is made from a frame of obsidian and lit with flint and steel.

Q16. Who created Minecraft?

Answer: Markus "Notch" Persson. Markus Persson made the first version of Minecraft in 2009.

Q17. In which year was the full version of Minecraft released?

Answer: 2011. Minecraft 1.0 was released in November 2011 at the MineCon event.

Q18. Which biome is home to the Warden?

Answer: The Deep Dark. The Warden is a blind creature that lurks in the Deep Dark.

Q19. What are the names of the two classic default skins in Minecraft?

Answer: Steve and Alex. Steve was the original default skin and Alex was added in 2014.

Q20. What is the highest level of the Sharpness enchantment in the standard game?

Answer: Five. Sharpness V is the maximum level available for a sword in normal survival.

About the Minecraft quiz

Minecraft has no single storyline, which is exactly what makes its trivia so distinctive — it is a quiz about a world's rules rather than a plot. Blocks, mobs, crafting recipes, biomes, and the strange logic of the Nether give players a shared vocabulary that runs surprisingly deep. Casual players know creepers and diamonds; devoted builders can recite crafting recipes, name every hostile mob, and explain how redstone works. A good Minecraft quiz rewards the players who spent enough hours in the world to learn it by heart.

Questions cover survival and creative play — crafting recipes, mobs like creepers and the Ender Dragon, biomes, ores, redstone, and the Nether and the End dimensions.

What this Minecraft quiz covers

This quiz draws from the game's systems — the mobs and how they behave, the blocks and materials, the crafting recipes, the biomes and dimensions, and the mechanics that make survival work. Expect a spread from the things every player knows to deeper detail. The early questions cover the basics; the harder ones reward people who have mastered the game's rules.
