Naruto Trivia Quiz

Naruto follows Naruto Uzumaki, a loud, mischievous ninja from the Hidden Leaf Village who has the powerful Nine-Tailed Fox sealed inside him and dreams of becoming Hokage, the village's leader. Alongside teammates Sasuke and Sakura under their teacher Kakashi, he trains, fights, and fights to win the respect of a village that once feared him.

Q1. What is Naruto Uzumaki's dream?

Answer: To become Hokage. Naruto wants to become Hokage so that the village will finally acknowledge him.

Q2. Which creature is sealed inside Naruto?

Answer: The Nine-Tailed Fox. The Nine-Tailed Fox, Kurama, was sealed in Naruto as a baby.

Q3. Which ramen shop does Naruto love to eat at?

Answer: Ichiraku Ramen. Ichiraku Ramen is Naruto's favourite place to eat, and its owner Teuchi treats him kindly.

Q4. Who is Naruto's rival and best friend?

Answer: Sasuke Uchiha. Sasuke is Naruto's teammate and rival, and their bond drives the story.

Q5. Who is the sensei who leads Team 7?

Answer: Kakashi Hatake. Kakashi teaches Naruto, Sasuke and Sakura the value of teamwork.

Q6. Which technique lets Naruto make multiple solid copies of himself?

Answer: Shadow Clone Jutsu. Shadow Clone Jutsu is Naruto's favourite technique, and the clones can share memories.

Q7. What is Sasuke's signature lightning technique?

Answer: Chidori. Kakashi teaches Sasuke Chidori, a technique that concentrates lightning chakra in the hand.

Q8. Which eye power does the Uchiha clan possess?

Answer: Sharingan. The Sharingan lets its user copy techniques and see through illusions.

Q9. Which of the legendary Sannin trains Naruto?

Answer: Jiraiya. Jiraiya, the Toad Sage, trains Naruto and teaches him Rasengan.

Q10. Who is Naruto and Sasuke's female teammate on Team 7?

Answer: Sakura Haruno. Sakura is a medical ninja who later trains under Tsunade.

Q11. Which clan has the Byakugan?

Answer: The Hyuga clan. The Byakugan lets Hyuga clan members see chakra and 360 degrees around them.

Q12. Who becomes the Fifth Hokage?

Answer: Tsunade. Tsunade, one of the Sannin, takes the title of Fifth Hokage and is known for her healing skills.

Q13. Who is Naruto's father?

Answer: Minato Namikaze. Minato, the Fourth Hokage, sealed the Nine-Tails inside his own son.

Q14. Which criminal organization wears black cloaks with red clouds?

Answer: Akatsuki. Akatsuki are S-rank criminals that hunt the Tailed Beasts.

Q15. Who is the jinchuriki of the One-Tailed Shukaku?

Answer: Gaara. Gaara, the Kazekage of the Hidden Sand, carries the One-Tail.

Q16. Who leads the Akatsuki when it attacks the Hidden Leaf Village?

Answer: Pain. Pain, the leader, destroys the Hidden Leaf Village before Naruto stops him.

Q17. What is the name of Naruto's mother?

Answer: Kushina Uzumaki. Kushina was the second jinchuriki of the Nine-Tails, and she had red hair and a fiery temper.

Q18. Which rotating technique does Naruto learn during his training with Jiraiya?

Answer: Rasengan. Rasengan is a spinning ball of chakra created by the Fourth Hokage.

Q19. Which Hidden Leaf ninja is a taijutsu specialist who cannot use ninjutsu?

Answer: Rock Lee. Rock Lee works hard to master taijutsu under the guidance of Might Guy.

Q20. Which village is Gaara's home?

Answer: Sunagakure, the Hidden Sand Village. Gaara becomes the Fifth Kazekage of Sunagakure.

About the Naruto quiz

Naruto is a long series with a huge cast, layered jutsu, and a story that spans two generations, all of which makes it a trivia favourite. The appeal is the mix: the emotional beats everyone remembers, the flashy techniques, and the clan histories that only committed fans track. Casual viewers know Naruto, Sasuke, and the Nine-Tails; the dedicated can explain the difference between jutsu types, name the Hokage in order, and trace the rivalries that drive the whole saga. A good Naruto quiz rewards both the heart of the story and the encyclopedic detail fans love to argue about.

Questions cover the Hidden Leaf and beyond — Team 7, the Hokage, the major clans like the Uchiha, the Chunin Exams, the tailed beasts, jutsu, and the long rivalry between Naruto and Sasuke.

What this Naruto quiz covers

Questions draw from Naruto's journey — the main characters and their teams, the villages and clans, the jutsu and their types, the Hokage, and the major arcs and rivalries. Expect a spread from the widely loved moments to the deeper lore. The early questions cover what most viewers remember; the harder ones reward people who followed the series closely across its long run.

Naruto quiz — frequently asked questions

Does this include the sequel series?

The focus is on Naruto's own story. Some questions may touch characters who carry into the next generation, but knowledge of the sequel series is not required.

Do I need to know all the jutsu?

No. A few questions touch techniques, but you do not need to memorise every jutsu — general familiarity with the characters and the story is enough to enjoy the quiz.

How much of the series should I have watched?

The more the better, since the questions range across the full journey. Even so, the early questions are friendly to anyone who knows the main characters and the setup.
