The Wire Trivia Quiz

The Wire is HBO's sprawling Baltimore crime drama that examines the city one institution at a time — the drug trade, the police, the docks, City Hall, the schools, and the newspaper. Across five seasons it follows detective Jimmy McNulty and a struggling police unit as they chase the Barksdale and Stanfield organisations, while showing how the same broken systems trap cops and corner kids alike.

Q1. In which US city is The Wire set?

Answer: Baltimore. The show is based in Baltimore and follows the city through different institutions.

Q2. Which network first aired The Wire?

Answer: HBO. The Wire ran on HBO from 2002 to 2008.

Q3. Who is the mayor of Baltimore at the start of the series?

Answer: Clarence Royce. Royce is the mayor who is facing an election while the police investigate the Barksdale group.

Q4. Which robber is famous for stealing from drug dealers?

Answer: Omar Little. Omar is a lone stick-up artist with a strict code.

Q5. Who is the leader of the Barksdale drug organisation?

Answer: Avon Barksdale. Avon is the boss of a large Baltimore drug operation, and is the target of the first police investigation.

Q6. Who is Avon Barksdale's right-hand man who studies economics?

Answer: Stringer Bell. Stringer runs the business and takes classes at a community college.

Q7. Which young gang leader takes over the drug trade in later seasons?

Answer: Marlo Stanfield. Marlo takes control of the western side of Baltimore by force and fear.

Q8. Who is McNulty's partner and friend in the homicide unit?

Answer: William "Bunk" Moreland. Bunk is a sharp detective who works with McNulty on a number of cases.

Q9. Which patient detective builds miniature furniture in his spare time?

Answer: Lester Freamon. Lester is a skilled and patient investigator who builds tiny furniture as a hobby.

Q10. Which ambitious city councilman runs for mayor of Baltimore?

Answer: Tommy Carcetti. Tommy Carcetti wants power and wins the election, but then finds it hard to run the city.

Q11. Which union leader is at the centre of season two on the docks?

Answer: Frank Sobotka. Frank is a stevedores' union leader who gets involved with smugglers to help his union.

Q12. Which four boys are at the centre of season four?

Answer: Namond, Randy, Michael and Dukie. Season four follows four schoolboys at the same middle school, and examines the education system.

Q13. Which nursery tune does Omar whistle when he is near?

Answer: "The Farmer in the Dell". Omar's whistling is a sign of trouble and makes the street clear.

Q14. Which lieutenant leads the detail investigating the Barksdale organisation?

Answer: Cedric Daniels. Daniels is an honest officer who tries to lead the detail while facing pressure from above.

Q15. Which season of The Wire focuses on the port of Baltimore?

Answer: Season two. Season two follows the dockworkers and the smuggling of goods through the port.

Q16. Who created The Wire?

Answer: David Simon. David Simon is a former Baltimore Sun reporter who created the show, and co-wrote it with Ed Burns.

Q17. In which years was The Wire first broadcast?

Answer: 2002 to 2008. The show ran for five seasons on HBO.

Q18. Who is Avon Barksdale's nephew who works in the low-rise projects?

Answer: D'Angelo Barksdale. D'Angelo is a thoughtful young man who runs part of the operation but has doubts about the life.

Q19. Which state senator is known for his drawn-out "sheeeeit"?

Answer: Clay Davis. Clay Davis is a corrupt and charming senator, who uses his catchphrase whenever he is accused.

Q20. Which season focuses on the newspaper?

Answer: Season five. The final season looks at the Baltimore Sun and its coverage of the city.

About the The Wire quiz

The Wire built a whole city out of interlocking institutions — the police, the streets, the docks, the schools, the press — and following those threads is exactly what makes it such demanding trivia. The show rewards attention like few others, with a huge cast and storylines that pay off seasons later. Casual viewers remember the main players and the biggest moments; devoted fans can trace the chain of command, name the corner players, and connect events across the seasons. A good quiz here rewards the viewers who treated Baltimore like a real place worth understanding.

Questions span all five seasons — McNulty, Bunk and the detail, Avon Barksdale and Stringer Bell, Omar Little, Marlo Stanfield, the dockworkers, the Hamsterdam experiment, the schools, and the Baltimore Sun — covering the characters, the catchphrases, and the moments that made the show.

What this The Wire quiz covers

This quiz draws from the series — the police and the street organisations, the central characters on both sides, the institutions the show examines, and the storylines that span its seasons. Expect a spread from the widely known moments to the deeper detail. The early questions cover what most viewers remember; the harder ones reward people who followed the show closely.
