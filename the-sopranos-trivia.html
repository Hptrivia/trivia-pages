The Sopranos Trivia Quiz

The Sopranos follows Tony Soprano, a New Jersey mob boss juggling two families — his crew in the DiMeo crime organisation and his wife and kids at home — while secretly seeing a psychiatrist for panic attacks. Over six seasons the HBO drama blends gangster violence with dark comedy and family dysfunction, redefining what television could do.

Q1. Who is the main character of The Sopranos?

Answer: Tony Soprano. Tony is a mob boss in New Jersey who tries to balance his family and his crew.

Q2. In which US state is The Sopranos set?

Answer: New Jersey. The show takes place in New Jersey and often features the suburbs.

Q3. Which actor plays Tony Soprano?

Answer: James Gandolfini. James Gandolfini won three Emmy awards for playing Tony.

Q4. Who is Tony's therapist?

Answer: Dr. Jennifer Melfi. Tony starts therapy with Dr. Melfi after having panic attacks.

Q5. What is the name of Tony's wife?

Answer: Carmela. Carmela Soprano is a strong-willed woman who struggles with her husband's life.

Q6. What is the name of the strip club that Tony uses as a base?

Answer: The Bada Bing. The Bada Bing is a club that acts as the crew's headquarters.

Q7. Who is Tony's young relative and an aspiring screenwriter?

Answer: Christopher Moltisanti. Christopher is an ambitious member of the crew who dreams of making it in movies.

Q8. What is the name of Tony's daughter?

Answer: Meadow. Meadow Soprano is a smart teenager who heads off to college during the series.

Q9. What is the name of Tony's son?

Answer: Anthony Jr. (A.J.). A.J. Soprano is a moody teenager who struggles to find his own way.

Q10. What is the name of Tony's controlling mother?

Answer: Livia. Livia Soprano is a difficult and manipulative woman who shapes Tony's issues.

Q11. What is the name of Tony's uncle who is a nominal boss?

Answer: Corrado "Junior" Soprano. Junior is Tony's uncle and a rival, and is a powerful figure of the family.

Q12. Which animals visit Tony's pool at the start of the series?

Answer: Ducks. The ducks affect Tony deeply, and they start his discussion in therapy.

Q13. Who is Tony's consigliere and the manager of the Bada Bing?

Answer: Silvio Dante. Silvio runs the club and is known for his impression of Al Pacino.

Q14. What is Paulie Gualtieri's nickname?

Answer: Walnuts. Paulie is a loyal, superstitious member of Tony's crew and is known as Paulie Walnuts.

Q15. What is the name of Artie Bucco's restaurant where the family often eats?

Answer: Nuovo Vesuvio. Artie is Tony's childhood friend and a chef at the Italian restaurant.

Q16. In which year did The Sopranos premiere?

Answer: 1999. The show premiered on HBO in January 1999.

Q17. How many seasons did The Sopranos run for?

Answer: Six. The series ran for six seasons from 1999 to 2007.

Q18. Which network broadcast The Sopranos?

Answer: HBO. The Sopranos helped to establish HBO as a home for quality drama.

Q19. Which Journey song plays during the final scene of the series?

Answer: "Don't Stop Believin'". The song plays in the diner scene that famously ends with a cut to black.

Q20. Which actor plays Carmela Soprano?

Answer: Edie Falco. Edie Falco won three Emmy awards for playing Carmela.

About the The Sopranos quiz

The Sopranos redefined television drama, and its blend of family, crime, and psychology gives trivia an unusually rich vein to mine. The show is dense with characters, relationships, and small details that reward close viewing. Casual watchers remember Tony, the therapy sessions, and the biggest moments; devoted fans can name the crew, trace the shifting loyalties, and recall the small touches that made the series a landmark. A good quiz here rewards the people who studied the family as closely as Tony's own psychiatrist did.

Questions cover all six seasons — Tony, Carmela, Christopher and the crew, Dr. Melfi's therapy sessions, rivals like Phil Leotardo and Richie Aprile, classic episodes, and the famous cut-to-black ending — testing both casual viewers and devoted fans.

What this The Sopranos quiz covers

This quiz draws from the series — Tony and his family, the crew and their loyalties, the therapy sessions, the key events, and the details that made the show a classic. Expect a spread from the widely known moments to the finer detail. The early questions cover what most viewers remember; the harder ones reward close watchers.
