Seinfeld Trivia Quiz

Seinfeld is the famous comedy "about nothing," following stand-up comedian Jerry Seinfeld and his friends — the neurotic, perpetually scheming George Costanza, eccentric across-the-hall neighbour Kramer, and sharp ex-girlfriend Elaine — as they obsess over the tiny absurdities of everyday life in New York City.

Q1. What is Jerry Seinfeld's job in the show?

Answer: Stand-up comedian. Jerry plays a fictional version of himself, a stand-up comedian in New York.

Q2. Which character is famous for bursting through Jerry's apartment door?

Answer: Kramer. Kramer barges in without knocking, and his entrances are among the show's best-known gags.

Q3. What is Elaine's last name?

Answer: Benes. Elaine Benes is Jerry's ex-girlfriend and friend.

Q4. Which phrase is shouted by the Soup Nazi in a famous episode?

Answer: "No soup for you!". The soup seller says this line to customers who break his rules.

Q5. Which network first aired Seinfeld?

Answer: NBC. Seinfeld ran on NBC and became one of the most popular sitcoms in history.

Q6. What false name does George often use to impress people?

Answer: Art Vandelay. George uses Art Vandelay as an architect and importer-exporter.

Q7. Which holiday is invented by George's father, Frank?

Answer: Festivus. Festivus is a holiday which is celebrated with an aluminium pole and the airing of grievances.

Q8. What is Newman's job?

Answer: Postal worker. Newman is a mailman who is Jerry's nemesis.

Q9. What is the number of Jerry's apartment?

Answer: 5A. Jerry lives in Apartment 5A in Manhattan, and Kramer lives across the hall.

Q10. What is the name of the diner where the friends often eat?

Answer: Monk's Café. The gang meets at Monk's Café to talk about their problems in almost every episode.

Q11. What is the title of Kramer's idea for a book about coffee tables?

Answer: The Coffee Table Book of Coffee Tables. Kramer's idea is a coffee table book that has legs and can be turned into a coffee table.

Q12. What is the name of Kramer's fictional company?

Answer: Kramerica Industries. Kramerica is the company that Kramer says he owns.

Q13. Where does Elaine work in the early seasons?

Answer: Pendant Publishing. Elaine works at Pendant Publishing before she is fired and joins J. Peterman.

Q14. What are the names of Jerry's parents?

Answer: Morty and Helen. Morty and Helen Seinfeld retire to Florida and appear in many episodes.

Q15. Which phrase does Frank Costanza use to calm himself down?

Answer: "Serenity now!". Frank uses "Serenity now!" as a calming mantra, though he ends up yelling it.

Q16. Who co-created Seinfeld with Jerry Seinfeld?

Answer: Larry David. Larry David and Jerry Seinfeld came up with the idea for a show about nothing.

Q17. In which year did Seinfeld premiere?

Answer: 1989. The show premiered in July 1989.

Q18. How many seasons did Seinfeld run for?

Answer: Nine. The show ended in 1998 after nine seasons.

Q19. How does the series finale end for the four friends?

Answer: They are sent to jail. The finale has all four friends convicted for their behaviour, and they end up in jail.

Q20. Which actor plays George Costanza?

Answer: Jason Alexander. Jason Alexander played the neurotic George for all nine seasons.

About the Seinfeld quiz

Seinfeld turned the small stuff into an art form, and that focus on tiny, specific details is exactly what makes its trivia so satisfying. The show about nothing is packed with catchphrases, minor characters, and running bits that regular viewers know by heart. Casual watchers remember the core four and a few famous episodes; devoted fans can name the recurring characters, recall the specific gripes each episode was built on, and quote the lines that entered the language. A good quiz here rewards the people who noticed how much the show got out of so little.

Questions cover the show about nothing — Jerry, George, Elaine, and Kramer, their New York haunts, the running gags, and famous episodes like "The Contest" and "The Soup Nazi."
