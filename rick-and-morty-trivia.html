Rick and Morty Trivia Quiz

Rick and Morty follows cynical, alcoholic super-genius Rick Sanchez and his anxious teenage grandson Morty as they portal between dimensions and alien worlds, usually dragging Morty's family — Beth, Jerry, and Summer — into the fallout of Rick's inventions.

Q1. Which device does Rick use to travel between dimensions?

Answer: A portal gun. Rick's green portal gun lets him travel to any dimension and planet.

Q2. How is Rick related to Morty?

Answer: He is Morty's grandfather. Rick is Beth's father and the grandfather of Morty and Summer.

Q3. Who is Morty's father?

Answer: Jerry Smith. Jerry is Beth's husband and often finds himself out of his depth in Rick's world.

Q4. Which pair created Rick and Morty?

Answer: Justin Roiland and Dan Harmon. The two co-created the show, which premiered in 2013.

Q5. What does Rick's catchphrase "Wubba lubba dub dub" actually mean, according to Birdperson?

Answer: "I am in great pain, please help me". Rick's catchphrase is revealed to be a cry for help, which hints at his inner turmoil.

Q6. Which blue creatures appear when a button is pressed and exist only to complete a task?

Answer: Mr. Meeseeks. Mr. Meeseeks are summoned with a box, and they start to suffer if a task is hard.

Q7. Which version of Morty seeks to overthrow the Citadel of Ricks?

Answer: Evil Morty. Evil Morty is a Morty who has an eyepatch and a plan of his own.

Q8. Into what does Rick turn himself in a famous episode?

Answer: A pickle. Pickle Rick is the famous episode in which Rick turns himself into a pickle to avoid family therapy.

Q9. What is Beth Smith's job?

Answer: She is a horse surgeon. Beth works as a surgeon for horses, and this is a source of pride and jokes.

Q10. What is Rick's dimension designation in the main show?

Answer: C-137. Rick C-137 is the version of Rick who Morty follows.

Q11. What is the name of the Smith family dog?

Answer: Snuffles. Snuffles is the family dog, and he is given intelligence in the episode "Lawnmower Dog".

Q12. Which cat-like friend of Rick says "squanch" all the time?

Answer: Squanchy. Squanchy is a cat-like alien who uses the word "squanch" to mean anything.

Q13. What is the name of the theme park inside a human body?

Answer: Anatomy Park. Anatomy Park is set inside a man named Reuben, and it starts out as an idea from Rick.

Q14. What is the name of the city where thousands of Ricks and Mortys live?

Answer: The Citadel of Ricks. The Citadel is a hidden city that is home to all the Ricks who have left their dimensions.

Q15. What must Earth's citizens do in the episode "Get Schwifty" to stay alive?

Answer: Sing a song. A giant floating head demands a great song from Earth, and Rick and Morty write "Get Schwifty".

Q16. What is the name of Rick's wife, who was killed?

Answer: Diane. Diane is Rick's wife and the mother of Beth, and her death is a major driver of his story.

Q17. Which network first aired Rick and Morty?

Answer: Adult Swim. The show is part of Adult Swim's line-up on Cartoon Network.

Q18. In which year did Rick and Morty premiere?

Answer: 2013. The pilot aired in December 2013.

Q19. What kind of company does Jerry Smith work at during the early seasons?

Answer: An advertising agency. Jerry works in advertising, and he often struggles with his career.

Q20. What is Rick called when he places his mind in a teenage clone body?

Answer: Tiny Rick. Tiny Rick is a youthful clone body that Rick uses to go to school as a teenager.

About the Rick and Morty quiz

Rick and Morty packs an absurd amount of invention into every episode — infinite timelines, throwaway characters, and deep-cut references that fans dissect for months. That density is exactly what makes it such rewarding trivia. Casual viewers remember Rick's catchphrases and the biggest episodes; devoted fans can name a one-off creature, track the multiverse rules, and recall the running gags buried in the background. A strong quiz here balances the moments everyone quotes with the chaotic detail that keeps the show's fandom endlessly busy.

Questions cover the family and the multiverse — Rick's gadgets and portal gun, the Citadel of Ricks, recurring characters like Mr. Meeseeks, and the show's most talked-about episodes.

What this Rick and Morty quiz covers

This quiz draws from across the series — Rick, Morty, and the family, the recurring characters and creatures, the multiverse rules, the catchphrases, and the standout episodes. Expect a mix from the widely known moments to the deeper cuts. The early questions cover what most viewers remember; the harder ones reward fans who pay close attention to the details.
