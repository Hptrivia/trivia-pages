Harry Potter Trivia Quiz

Harry Potter follows an orphaned boy who learns on his eleventh birthday that he is a wizard and is whisked off to Hogwarts School of Witchcraft and Wizardry. Sorted into Gryffindor, he befriends Ron Weasley and Hermione Granger and slowly uncovers his connection to the dark wizard Lord Voldemort, who murdered his parents and left him with a lightning-bolt scar.

Q1. Which platform at King's Cross station do students use to board the Hogwarts Express?

Answer: Platform 9¾. Students reach Platform 9¾ by walking through the barrier between platforms 9 and 10.

Q2. What position does Harry play on the Gryffindor Quidditch team?

Answer: Seeker. Harry is the youngest Seeker in a century, chasing the Golden Snitch.

Q3. Which Hogwarts house is Harry sorted into?

Answer: Gryffindor. The Sorting Hat places Harry in Gryffindor after he asks not to be put in Slytherin.

Q4. What is the name of Harry's snowy owl?

Answer: Hedwig. Hagrid gives Hedwig to Harry as an eleventh birthday present.

Q5. Who is the gamekeeper who tells Harry, "You're a wizard"?

Answer: Rubeus Hagrid. Hagrid arrives on Harry's eleventh birthday to bring him news of his magical heritage.

Q6. What is the core of Harry's wand?

Answer: Phoenix feather. Harry's holly wand contains a feather from Fawkes, Dumbledore's phoenix.

Q7. Which potion do Harry and Ron use to disguise themselves in Chamber of Secrets?

Answer: Polyjuice Potion. Polyjuice Potion lets the drinker take another person's appearance for an hour.

Q8. What is the name of the three-headed dog guarding the Philosopher's Stone?

Answer: Fluffy. Hagrid's dog Fluffy is put to sleep by music, which is how Harry and friends get past.

Q9. Who teaches Potions at Hogwarts during Harry's first year?

Answer: Severus Snape. Snape is Potions master and head of Slytherin House when Harry arrives.

Q10. What is the name of the wizarding bank run by goblins?

Answer: Gringotts. Gringotts is located in Diagon Alley, and its vaults are protected by dragons and other guards.

Q11. Which spell conjures the Dark Mark in the sky?

Answer: Morsmordre. Death Eaters cast Morsmordre to signal a killing, as seen after the Quidditch World Cup.

Q12. Which member of the Marauders is known as Padfoot?

Answer: Sirius Black. Sirius turned into a large black dog, which earned him the nickname Padfoot.

Q13. Which ghost haunts Slytherin House?

Answer: The Bloody Baron. The Bloody Baron is the silent, blood-stained ghost of Slytherin, and only Peeves fears him.

Q14. What is the name of Fred and George Weasley's joke shop?

Answer: Weasleys' Wizard Wheezes. The twins open their shop in Diagon Alley using Harry's Triwizard winnings.

Q15. What is the name of Neville Longbottom's pet toad?

Answer: Trevor. Neville keeps losing Trevor, who hops around the Hogwarts Express in the first book.

Q16. Who destroys Slytherin's locket Horcrux with the Sword of Gryffindor?

Answer: Ron Weasley. Ron destroys the locket after Harry retrieves the sword from a frozen pool.

Q17. Which spell is labelled "for enemies" in the Half-Blood Prince's textbook?

Answer: Sectumsempra. Snape invented Sectumsempra, which Harry later uses on Draco Malfoy.

Q18. How many children do Arthur and Molly Weasley have?

Answer: Seven. Bill, Charlie, Percy, Fred, George, Ron and Ginny are the seven Weasley children.

Q19. Which of these is one of Albus Dumbledore's middle names?

Answer: Wulfric. His full name is Albus Percival Wulfric Brian Dumbledore.

Q20. What is the real name of the Grey Lady, the ghost of Ravenclaw?

Answer: Helena Ravenclaw. Helena, the daughter of Rowena Ravenclaw, is revealed as the Grey Lady in the final book.

About the Harry Potter quiz

Few stories reward re-reading the way Harry Potter does, which is exactly why it makes such rich trivia. J.K. Rowling seeded the seven books with small details that only pay off chapters or volumes later — a throwaway line in the first book that turns out to matter in the last. Casual fans remember the houses, the wands, and the big duels; the real experts remember which professor taught which subject in which year, the exact wording of a prophecy, and who ended up with whom. A good Harry Potter quiz lives in that gap between watching the films once and knowing the wizarding world inside out.

Questions range across all seven books and eight films — the four Hogwarts houses and their founders, Quidditch, the professors, magical creatures, the Triwizard Tournament, Horcruxes, and the Order of the Phoenix's long battle against Voldemort and the Death Eaters.

What this Harry Potter quiz covers

This quiz draws from all seven books and eight films, so expect a spread rather than a single-movie focus. Questions touch the four Hogwarts houses and their founders, core characters like Harry, Ron, and Hermione, the professors and their subjects, Quidditch, magical creatures, the Triwizard Tournament, Horcruxes, and the long war against Voldemort and the Death Eaters. Early questions stick to the things most fans know; later ones lean on the kind of detail you only pick up after several re-reads.

Harry Potter quiz — frequently asked questions

Do I need to have read all seven books to do well?

Not to start. The opening questions cover the widely known parts of the series — the houses, the main trio, and the biggest moments — that most people know from the films alone. The later questions do reward book readers, since some answers come from details the films trimmed or skipped.

Are the questions from the books or the movies?

Both. The quiz treats the wizarding world as one continuous story, so a question might come from a scene that appears in both, or from something that only made it into the books. Where the two versions differ, the answer follows the books.

Is this quiz spoiler-heavy?

It assumes you know the broad shape of the story, so a few answers touch major events. If you have finished the series — in either format — nothing here will spoil anything you haven't already seen.
