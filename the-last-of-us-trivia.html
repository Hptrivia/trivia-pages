About the The Last of Us quiz

The Last of Us tells one of the most emotionally precise stories in gaming, and that focus is what makes its trivia so rewarding. The journey, the characters, and the world's harsh rules are all tightly drawn, so the details stick. Casual fans remember Joel, Ellie, and the broad arc; devoted players can name the locations, trace the journey's stages, and recall the small moments that give the story its weight. A good quiz here rewards the people who felt every beat of the journey and remembered where it took them.

Questions cover the journey — Joel and Ellie, the stages of the infected, factions like the Fireflies, and the moments that made the story so talked-about.

What this The Last of Us quiz covers

This quiz draws from the story — the central characters, the journey and its locations, the world's dangers and factions, and the moments that define the arc. Expect a spread from the widely known beats to the finer detail. The early questions cover what most fans remember; the harder ones reward people who know the story closely.

How to play

This The Last of Us quiz runs 20 multiple-choice questions, each with four options and one correct answer. Tap an option to lock it in and see straight away whether you got it right, watch your score climb as you go, and get a final total at the end.
