The Last of Us Trivia Quiz

The Last of Us is set roughly twenty years after a mutated Cordyceps fungus collapsed civilization and turned the infected into monsters. It follows a hardened smuggler named Joel who is hired to escort Ellie, a teenage girl who may be immune, across a dangerous, ruined America.

Q1. Which smuggler is hired to escort Ellie across a ruined America?

Answer: Joel Miller. Joel is a hardened survivor who begins the journey as a job and ends as a father figure.

Q2. Which type of fungus causes the outbreak that destroys civilisation?

Answer: Cordyceps. The fungus takes over the brains of its hosts and turns them into monsters.

Q3. What are the infected who navigate by making clicking sounds called?

Answer: Clickers. Clickers are blind, so they use echolocation and are deadly at close range.

Q4. What is the name of Joel's daughter, whom he loses at the beginning?

Answer: Sarah. The story opens with Joel's daughter Sarah on the night the outbreak begins.

Q5. What is the name of the resistance group that hopes to find a cure?

Answer: The Fireflies. The Fireflies fight the government and hope to use Ellie to develop a cure.

Q6. In which city do Joel and Ellie begin their journey?

Answer: Boston. The pair leave the Boston quarantine zone and head west across the country.

Q7. Who is Joel's smuggling partner at the start?

Answer: Tess. Tess is tough and loyal, and she helps Joel to smuggle goods in Boston.

Q8. Who is Joel's younger brother?

Answer: Tommy. Tommy left Boston years before and is a former soldier who joined the Fireflies.

Q9. Which paranoid survivalist has booby-trapped his town?

Answer: Bill. Bill is a grumpy loner who has made the town a fortress, and is not fond of visitors.

Q10. In which town in Wyoming is Tommy living when Joel finds him?

Answer: Jackson. Jackson is a working settlement, and it has power and a safe wall.

Q11. In which city is the Fireflies' hospital at the end of the story?

Answer: Salt Lake City. The Fireflies take Ellie to a hospital in Salt Lake City, where the story's biggest choice is made.

Q12. What is the name of the leader of the Fireflies who is a friend of Ellie's mother?

Answer: Marlene. Marlene promised Ellie's mother to look after her and asks Joel to deliver her.

Q13. What is the name of the boy who travels with his older brother Henry?

Answer: Sam. Sam and Henry travel with Joel and Ellie through Pittsburgh.

Q14. Which type of infected is a huge, armoured monster that throws spore-filled growths?

Answer: Bloater. Bloaters are found in later stages of infection and are very hard to defeat.

Q15. Which cult leader hunts Ellie during the winter?

Answer: David. David leads a group of survivors and is a dangerous man, hiding behind a calm manner.

Q16. Which studio developed the original The Last of Us game?

Answer: Naughty Dog. Naughty Dog, based in California, also made Uncharted.

Q17. In which year was the original game released?

Answer: 2013. The Last of Us was released in June 2013 on the PlayStation 3.

Q18. Which actor plays Joel in the HBO series?

Answer: Pedro Pascal. Pedro Pascal starred in the first season, which was released in 2023.

Q19. Which actor plays Ellie in the HBO series?

Answer: Bella Ramsey. Bella Ramsey plays Ellie in the HBO adaptation.

Q20. Who composed the acoustic score for the game?

Answer: Gustavo Santaolalla. Gustavo Santaolalla is known for his quiet guitar sound, which gives the story a sad tone.

About the The Last of Us quiz

The Last of Us tells one of the most emotionally precise stories in gaming, and that focus is what makes its trivia so rewarding. The journey, the characters, and the world's harsh rules are all tightly drawn, so the details stick. Casual fans remember Joel, Ellie, and the broad arc; devoted players can name the locations, trace the journey's stages, and recall the small moments that give the story its weight. A good quiz here rewards the people who felt every beat of the journey and remembered where it took them.

Questions cover the journey — Joel and Ellie, the stages of the infected, factions like the Fireflies, and the moments that made the story so talked-about.

What this The Last of Us quiz covers

This quiz draws from the story — the central characters, the journey and its locations, the world's dangers and factions, and the moments that define the arc. Expect a spread from the widely known beats to the finer detail. The early questions cover what most fans remember; the harder ones reward people who know the story closely.
