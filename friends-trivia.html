Friends Trivia Quiz

Friends follows six twenty- and thirty-somethings — Ross, Rachel, Monica, Chandler, Joey, and Phoebe — sharing apartments and hanging out at the Central Perk coffee house in Manhattan. Across ten seasons it tracks their careers, roommates, and tangled love lives, above all the on-again, off-again romance between Ross and Rachel.

Q1. What is the name of the coffee house where the friends hang out?

Answer: Central Perk. Central Perk, with its big orange couch, is the group's second home throughout all ten seasons.

Q2. What is the name of Ross's pet monkey?

Answer: Marcel. Marcel is the capuchin monkey Ross keeps in his apartment during the early seasons.

Q3. Which character writes and sings the song "Smelly Cat"?

Answer: Phoebe Buffay. Phoebe, the group's quirky guitar-playing masseuse, performs Smelly Cat at Central Perk.

Q4. What is Joey's famous pick-up line?

Answer: "How you doin'?". Joey Tribbiani uses "How you doin'?" as his signature greeting to women.

Q5. What does Ross do for a living?

Answer: He is a paleontologist. Ross studies dinosaurs and teaches at a New York university.

Q6. What is Chandler's job, which nobody can ever explain?

Answer: Statistical analysis and data reconfiguration. The running joke is that none of his friends can say what Chandler's job actually involves.

Q7. What is Monica Geller's profession?

Answer: She is a chef. Monica is a professional chef, and her perfectionism in the kitchen is a running gag.

Q8. What is Rachel's first job after she leaves her wedding?

Answer: Waitress at Central Perk. Rachel starts the series as a runaway bride and takes a waitressing job at Central Perk.

Q9. What is the name of Ross's first wife, who leaves him for another woman?

Answer: Carol. Carol Willick leaves Ross for her partner Susan, and the three later co-parent their son Ben.

Q10. What is the name of Phoebe's identical twin sister?

Answer: Ursula. Ursula Buffay is Phoebe's twin, a waitress whom Phoebe is often embarrassed by.

Q11. What does Chandler's mother, Nora Tyler Bing, do for a living?

Answer: She writes romance novels. Nora Tyler Bing is a bestselling romance novelist, much to Chandler's embarrassment.

Q12. In "The One with the Embryos", what contest costs Monica and Rachel their apartment?

Answer: A trivia game about each other. The guys win the apartment by out-answering the girls in a trivia game about their own lives.

Q13. What is the name of the character Joey plays on the soap opera Days of Our Lives?

Answer: Dr. Drake Ramoray. Joey lands his big break as brain surgeon Dr. Drake Ramoray.

Q14. Which actor plays Gunther, the Central Perk manager who is secretly in love with Rachel?

Answer: James Michael Tyler. James Michael Tyler played Gunther in nearly every season of the show.

Q15. What are the first names of Monica and Ross's parents?

Answer: Jack and Judy. Jack and Judy Geller are the parents of Ross and Monica, and Judy is famous for her criticism of Monica.

Q16. Which actor plays Chandler Bing?

Answer: Matthew Perry. Matthew Perry played Chandler, the group's sarcastic king of one-liners.

Q17. What is the name of the man Rachel leaves at the altar in the pilot episode?

Answer: Barry. Rachel runs out on her orthodontist fiancé Barry, which leads her to Central Perk.

Q18. Whose name does Ross mistakenly say at the altar when he marries Emily in London?

Answer: Rachel. Ross says "I, Ross, take thee Rachel", ending his wedding to Emily in disaster.

Q19. What is the name of Ross and Rachel's daughter?

Answer: Emma. Emma Geller-Green is born at the end of season 8 to Rachel and Ross.

Q20. Which actor plays Phoebe's husband Mike Hannigan?

Answer: Paul Rudd. Paul Rudd plays Mike, who becomes Phoebe's love interest in the later seasons.

About the Friends quiz

Friends ran for ten seasons and 236 episodes, which means the trivia well is deep and the running jokes are everywhere. Because so many people have rewatched it end to end, a good Friends quiz has to go beyond the obvious — everyone knows the coffee house and Ross yelling "we were on a break." The real test is remembering the guest stars, the middle names, the jobs each character drifted through, and the tiny recurring gags that only regular rewatchers catch. It is comfort-TV trivia at its best: familiar enough to be fun, detailed enough to trip you up.

Questions cover all ten seasons — Central Perk, Ross and Rachel's relationship, Monica and Chandler, Joey's acting career, Phoebe's songs, the Thanksgiving episodes, and the catchphrases that became pop culture.

What this Friends quiz covers

This quiz reaches across all ten seasons and the six main characters — Ross, Rachel, Monica, Chandler, Joey, and Phoebe. Expect questions on the big relationships, the running jokes, the jobs and apartments, the famous guest appearances, and the moments that became part of pop culture. The opening questions cover what any viewer remembers; the later ones reward the people who have seen every episode more than once.

Friends quiz — frequently asked questions

Is this only about the main six characters?

Mostly, but not entirely. The core cast drives most questions, though a few touch the recurring characters and famous guest stars who passed through over the ten seasons.

Do I need to remember specific episodes?

Rarely. Almost every question is about what happened and to whom. One or two may mention an episode title, but you can answer them from the story, so general familiarity with the series is enough.

How hard does it get?

The early questions are friendly for any fan. The later ones lean on details, such as the guest stars, the supporting characters and the one-off jokes, which reward people who know the show inside out.
