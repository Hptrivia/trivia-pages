The Mandalorian Trivia Quiz

The Mandalorian is set in the Star Wars galaxy after the fall of the Empire and follows Din Djarin, a lone, helmeted bounty hunter bound by a strict creed. His life changes when a bounty turns out to be a tiny, Force-sensitive child of Yoda's species — Grogu, nicknamed "Baby Yoda" — whom he chooses to protect.

Q1. What is the real name of the Mandalorian?

Answer: Din Djarin. Din Djarin is a lone bounty hunter who takes care of a mysterious child.

Q2. What is the fan nickname for Grogu?

Answer: Baby Yoda. Grogu is the same species as Yoda but is a child, and fans called him Baby Yoda.

Q3. What is the name of the metal used in Mandalorian armour?

Answer: Beskar. Beskar is a valuable metal that can stop a lightsaber and blaster fire.

Q4. What is the Mandalorians' famous phrase?

Answer: "This is the Way". The phrase is used to show devotion to the Mandalorian creed.

Q5. On which streaming service did The Mandalorian first premiere?

Answer: Disney+. The show was one of the first big series on Disney+ when it launched in 2019.

Q6. Who is the head of the Bounty Hunters' Guild?

Answer: Greef Karga. Greef Karga gives Din his first jobs, and later becomes a major ally.

Q7. Which former Rebel shock trooper becomes Din's friend?

Answer: Cara Dune. Cara Dune is a strong fighter who chooses to settle down on the planet Nevarro.

Q8. Which droid is reprogrammed to protect the Child?

Answer: IG-11. IG-11 begins as an assassin droid, but is changed to serve and protect Grogu.

Q9. Who is the head of Din Djarin's covert?

Answer: The Armorer. The Armorer forges beskar armour and leads the group of Mandalorians.

Q10. Who is the Imperial scientist that experiments on the Child?

Answer: Dr. Pershing. Dr. Pershing works for the remnants of the Empire and wants to study Grogu.

Q11. Which weapon does Moff Gideon carry?

Answer: The Darksaber. The Darksaber is an ancient blade with a black blade of light.

Q12. Which Jedi arrives to take Grogu at the end of season two?

Answer: Luke Skywalker. Luke arrives with R2-D2 to teach Grogu the ways of the Force.

Q13. Which actor plays Din Djarin?

Answer: Pedro Pascal. Pedro Pascal voices Din Djarin, and is seen without a helmet in a few scenes.

Q14. Who plays Greef Karga, the head of the Bounty Hunters' Guild?

Answer: Carl Weathers. Carl Weathers, a former athlete and Hollywood star, played Greef Karga.

Q15. Which famous German director plays the Client in season one?

Answer: Werner Herzog. Werner Herzog plays the Client, the man who sends Din Djarin on his first mission.

Q16. Which Star Wars bounty hunter gets a spin-off series after The Mandalorian?

Answer: Boba Fett. The Book of Boba Fett follows Boba after he takes over Jabba's old palace.

Q17. What is the name of the group of Mandalorians that follows the strict old creed?

Answer: The Children of the Watch. Din Djarin's covert follows an old creed which says helmets must never be removed in front of others.

Q18. In which year did The Mandalorian premiere?

Answer: 2019. The Mandalorian debuted in November 2019.

Q19. What is the name of the Ugnaught who helps Din Djarin?

Answer: Kuiil. Kuiil is a hard-working Ugnaught who helps Din to find the Child.

Q20. What creed forbids Din Djarin from removing his helmet in front of others?

Answer: The Way of the Mandalore. The Way is a set of rules from his covert that governs his life.

About the The Mandalorian quiz

The Mandalorian expanded the Star Wars galaxy into new corners while keeping a tight, character-driven story at its centre — a combination that makes for great trivia. The bounties, the creed, the ships, and the wider connections to the saga all give fans plenty to track. Casual viewers remember the helmet and the Child; devoted fans can name the planets, explain the Mandalorian code, and place the show's events within the larger timeline. A good quiz here rewards both the heartfelt central story and the galaxy of detail around it.

Questions cover the series — Din Djarin and the Mandalorian creed, Grogu, the bounties and allies, the planets they visit, and the ties to the wider Star Wars galaxy.
