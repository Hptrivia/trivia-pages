About the The Mandalorian quiz

The Mandalorian expanded the Star Wars galaxy into new corners while keeping a tight, character-driven story at its centre — a combination that makes for great trivia. The bounties, the creed, the ships, and the wider connections to the saga all give fans plenty to track. Casual viewers remember the helmet and the Child; devoted fans can name the planets, explain the Mandalorian code, and place the show's events within the larger timeline. A good quiz here rewards both the heartfelt central story and the galaxy of detail around it.

Questions cover the series — Din Djarin and the Mandalorian creed, Grogu, the bounties and allies, the planets they visit, and the ties to the wider Star Wars galaxy.
