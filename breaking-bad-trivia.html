Breaking Bad Trivia Quiz

Breaking Bad follows Walter White, a struggling high-school chemistry teacher in Albuquerque who, after a terminal lung cancer diagnosis, starts cooking crystal meth with his former student Jesse Pinkman to secure his family's future. Over five seasons he hardens into the ruthless drug kingpin known as Heisenberg, while his DEA brother-in-law Hank unknowingly hunts him.

Q1. What is Walter White's job at the start of the series?

Answer: High school chemistry teacher. Walter is an underpaid chemistry teacher in Albuquerque when he is diagnosed with lung cancer.

Q2. Which alias does Walter use in the drug trade?

Answer: Heisenberg. Walter takes the name Heisenberg, after the physicist, as he builds his criminal reputation.

Q3. In which US state is Breaking Bad set?

Answer: New Mexico. The series is filmed and set in and around Albuquerque, New Mexico.

Q4. Which actor plays Walter White?

Answer: Bryan Cranston. Bryan Cranston won multiple Emmy awards for playing Walter.

Q5. What is Hank Schrader's job?

Answer: He is a DEA agent. Hank is Walter's brother-in-law, and he hunts the mysterious drug maker known as Heisenberg.

Q6. What is the title of the Breaking Bad series finale?

Answer: Felina. The finale, "Felina", closes Walter's story at the end of season five.

Q7. Which car does Walter White drive at the start of the series?

Answer: A Pontiac Aztek. The unglamorous Pontiac Aztek matches Walter's humble life as a teacher.

Q8. What was the name of the company Walter helped found before leaving it?

Answer: Gray Matter Technologies. Walter co-founded Gray Matter with Elliott Schwartz, and he later regrets selling his stake.

Q9. What name does Walter Jr. prefer to be called?

Answer: Flynn. Walter Jr. asks to be called Flynn, and he loves eating breakfast.

Q10. What is the name of the fast-food restaurant chain run by Gus Fring?

Answer: Los Pollos Hermanos. Gus uses the friendly chicken restaurant as a front for his drug distribution.

Q11. Which former police officer works as Gus Fring's fixer?

Answer: Mike Ehrmantraut. Mike is a quiet, efficient former cop who later becomes Walter's partner.

Q12. What is the name of the car wash where Walter works part-time?

Answer: A1A Car Wash. Walter takes a second job at the A1A Car Wash to make ends meet in the opening episodes.

Q13. Which older character communicates using a bell?

Answer: Hector Salamanca. Hector uses a desk bell to spell out his replies after a stroke.

Q14. What does Hank Schrader brew at home as a hobby?

Answer: Beer. Hank's home-brewed beer is called Schraderbräu.

Q15. Complete Walter's famous line: "I am the one who ___".

Answer: knocks. Walter tells Skyler "I am the one who knocks", showing how much he has changed.

Q16. What does Walter throw onto his garage roof in the first season?

Answer: A pizza. Walter's angry pizza toss became one of the show's best-known moments.

Q17. What is the title of the sequel film that follows Jesse Pinkman after the series ends?

Answer: El Camino. El Camino: A Breaking Bad Movie was released in 2019 and follows Jesse's escape.

Q18. Which two chemical element symbols are highlighted in the title of the series?

Answer: Br and Ba. The title's Br and Ba stand for bromine and barium, and are highlighted in the show's logo.

Q19. Which actor plays Jesse Pinkman?

Answer: Aaron Paul. Aaron Paul won three Emmys for playing Jesse, Walter's former student and partner.

Q20. Which prequel series stars Bob Odenkirk as Jimmy McGill?

Answer: Better Call Saul. Better Call Saul follows the lawyer before he becomes Walter's counsel.

About the Breaking Bad quiz

Breaking Bad is one of the most tightly plotted shows ever made, and that precision is a gift for trivia. Nothing is wasted — a colour, a stuffed animal, a chemistry lesson — and the series rewards viewers who notice. Casual watchers remember Heisenberg, the RV, and the big set-pieces; superfans can name the exact substance behind a key death, track the money, and recall which alias Walt used and when. A strong Breaking Bad quiz tests both the surface story everyone knows and the small, deliberate details that made the show a modern classic.

Questions span all five seasons — Walt and Jesse's partnership, the rise of Heisenberg, Hank's DEA investigation, Gus Fring and the cartel, Saul Goodman, and the memorable lines and deaths along the way.

What this Breaking Bad quiz covers

Questions span all five seasons, from Walter White's diagnosis and first cook through his transformation into Heisenberg and the fallout that follows. Expect the core characters — Walt, Jesse, Skyler, Hank, Saul, Gus — plus the key locations, the aliases, the methods, and the turning points that define each season. The early questions cover the widely known beats; the later ones reward viewers who paid attention to the details the show planted along the way.

Breaking Bad quiz — frequently asked questions

Does this include the spin-off and the movie?

Mostly not. The quiz focuses on the main Breaking Bad series, but one or two expert questions touch the prequel Better Call Saul and the film El Camino. You can answer everything else without having seen either.

Do I need to have finished the show?

It helps. The questions draw from all five seasons, so a few answers touch late-series events. If you have watched to the end, nothing here will spoil you.

Are the questions about plot or small details?

Both. Some test the major storyline everyone remembers, while others reward attention to the smaller, deliberate details the show was famous for planting and paying off.
