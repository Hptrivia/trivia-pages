Pokémon Trivia Quiz

Pokémon follows Trainers who catch and battle creatures called Pokémon. It begins in the Kanto region, where you pick a starter — Bulbasaur, Charmander, or Squirtle — and set out to defeat eight Gym Leaders and the Elite Four to become Champion. Decades of games and the anime have since added new regions, new generations, and hundreds more creatures.

Q1. Which Pokémon is Ash Ketchum's most famous partner?

Answer: Pikachu. Ash receives Pikachu from Professor Oak, and it refuses to stay in its Poké Ball.

Q2. What type is Charmander?

Answer: Fire. Charmander is a Fire-type, and evolves first into Charmeleon and then Charizard.

Q3. Which item is used to catch wild Pokémon?

Answer: Poké Ball. Trainers throw a Poké Ball at a weakened wild Pokémon to try to capture it.

Q4. Which Pokémon does Magikarp evolve into?

Answer: Gyarados. Magikarp is weak, but at level 20 it evolves into the fearsome Gyarados.

Q5. Which professor gives trainers their first Pokémon in Pallet Town?

Answer: Professor Oak. Professor Samuel Oak is the Pokémon researcher who starts the journey of every Kanto trainer.

Q6. What are the three Kanto starter Pokémon?

Answer: Bulbasaur, Charmander and Squirtle. The choice of Bulbasaur, Charmander or Squirtle begins the original Red and Blue games.

Q7. What does Pikachu evolve into when exposed to a Thunder Stone?

Answer: Raichu. A Thunder Stone turns Pikachu into Raichu.

Q8. Which type is immune to Electric-type attacks?

Answer: Ground. Ground-type Pokémon take no damage from Electric moves.

Q9. Who is the Gym Leader of Pewter City?

Answer: Brock. Brock uses Rock-type Pokémon and is the first Gym Leader in the Kanto games.

Q10. Which Pokémon appears on the North American box art of Pokémon Red?

Answer: Charizard. Charizard is on Red, Blastoise on Blue, and Pikachu on Yellow.

Q11. How many Pokémon are in the original Pokédex of Generation I?

Answer: 151. The original Pokédex lists 151 Pokémon, ending with the mythical Mew.

Q12. Who is the boss of Team Rocket in the Kanto games?

Answer: Giovanni. Giovanni leads Team Rocket and is also the Gym Leader of Viridian City.

Q13. Which Eevee evolution is a Water-type?

Answer: Vaporeon. Vaporeon is the Water-type evolution of Eevee, reached with a Water Stone.

Q14. Kadabra evolves into which Psychic-type Pokémon when it is traded?

Answer: Alakazam. Kadabra becomes Alakazam through a trade, and Abra becomes Kadabra by levelling up.

Q15. Which region are Pokémon Gold and Silver set in?

Answer: Johto. Gold and Silver take place in Johto, which is right next to Kanto.

Q16. Which Pokémon is number 25 in the National Pokédex?

Answer: Pikachu. Pikachu holds Pokédex number 25, and Raichu is number 26.

Q17. What is Charizard's type combination?

Answer: Fire and Flying. Despite its dragon-like look, Charizard is a Fire and Flying type.

Q18. In Red and Blue, which rival does the player fight as the final Champion?

Answer: The rival, Blue. In the original games, the player's rival becomes the new Champion before you beat them at the Indigo Plateau.

Q19. What is Ash Ketchum's hometown?

Answer: Pallet Town. Ash and his rival Gary both live in Pallet Town, where Professor Oak's lab is.

Q20. Which three Legendary birds live in Kanto?

Answer: Articuno, Zapdos and Moltres. The Legendary birds of Kanto are the Ice-type Articuno, Electric-type Zapdos and Fire-type Moltres.

About the Pokémon quiz

Pokémon is almost designed for trivia. With hundreds of creatures, a type chart that plays like rock-paper-scissors on steroids, and decades of games and anime episodes, there is a near-endless supply of facts to test. What makes a great Pokémon quiz is balance: the fun of naming a starter or a Gym Leader sits right next to the deeper knowledge of evolution methods, type match-ups, and which region introduced what. Everyone who grew up with a Game Boy has a strong opinion about the best starter — but only the dedicated Trainers can rattle off the Elite Four in order.

Questions cover the games and the anime — starter and legendary Pokémon, type advantages, evolutions, Gym Leaders and the Elite Four, regions from Kanto onward, and Ash and Pikachu's journey.

What this Pokémon quiz covers

Questions here pull from both the games and the anime. Expect starters and legendaries, type advantages and weaknesses, evolutions and how they trigger, Gym Leaders and the Elite Four, the regions from Kanto onward, and the long journey of Ash and Pikachu. The early questions favour the first generation that most players remember; later ones range across the newer regions and the more technical corners of the battle system.

Pokémon quiz — frequently asked questions

Which generations does this quiz cover?

It starts in Kanto — the original games and the earliest anime — because that is common ground for almost everyone. From there it reaches into later generations and regions, so a broad knowledge of the series helps on the harder questions.

Is it based on the games or the show?

A mix of both. Some questions come straight from the games (type match-ups, evolution methods), while others come from the anime (Ash, his companions, and famous episodes). Where a fact appears in both, either source will get you the right answer.

Do I need to know competitive battling?

No. A few questions touch type advantages, but you do not need any competitive or breeding knowledge. General familiarity with the creatures and the world is enough to enjoy the quiz.
