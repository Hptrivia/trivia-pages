The Big Bang Theory Trivia Quiz

The Big Bang Theory follows a group of brilliant but socially awkward scientists — physicists Sheldon Cooper and Leonard Hofstadter and their friends Howard and Raj — whose routine of work, comic books, and takeout is upended when aspiring actress Penny moves in across the hall.

Q1. What is Sheldon Cooper's famous catchphrase after a prank or joke?

Answer: "Bazinga!". Sheldon shouts "Bazinga!" to reveal that he is joking.

Q2. At which university do the main characters work?

Answer: Caltech. The four scientists work at the California Institute of Technology in Pasadena.

Q3. Which character lives across the hall from Sheldon and Leonard?

Answer: Penny. Penny is a waitress and aspiring actress who befriends the physicists.

Q4. What does Sheldon call the seat on the couch that only he is allowed to use?

Answer: His spot. Sheldon insists his spot is the best seat, because of temperature, drafts and view of the TV.

Q5. Which actor plays Sheldon Cooper?

Answer: Jim Parsons. Jim Parsons won four Emmy awards for playing Sheldon.

Q6. Where does Penny work as a waitress?

Answer: The Cheesecake Factory. Penny works at the Cheesecake Factory before starting her career as a pharmaceutical rep.

Q7. What is Howard Wolowitz's profession?

Answer: Aerospace engineer. Howard is an engineer with a master's degree, which the others often tease him about.

Q8. Which game extends Rock Paper Scissors with two more options?

Answer: Rock Paper Scissors Lizard Spock. Sheldon explains that "Lizard poisons Spock", and other rules, in a memorable scene.

Q9. Which actor plays Penny?

Answer: Kaley Cuoco. Kaley Cuoco plays Penny, the neighbour who becomes a central character of the show.

Q10. What condition prevents Raj Koothrappali from talking to women?

Answer: Selective mutism. Raj cannot speak to women unless he has had alcohol, until his condition eases later in the series.

Q11. What is the name of Sheldon's twin sister?

Answer: Missy. Missy is Sheldon's fraternal twin sister, who is much more sociable.

Q12. What is the name of the comic book shop in Pasadena that the friends visit?

Answer: The Comic Center of Pasadena. Stuart runs the store and is often quietly lonely.

Q13. What is the name of Sheldon's mother?

Answer: Mary Cooper. Mary is a devout Texan mother, and her family life is the basis of Young Sheldon.

Q14. What is Leonard Hofstadter's profession?

Answer: Experimental physicist. Leonard is an experimental physicist, while Sheldon is a theorist.

Q15. What is the name of Leonard's cold, analytical mother?

Answer: Beverly Hofstadter. Beverly treats her son like a research subject rather than a child.

Q16. In which city is The Big Bang Theory set?

Answer: Pasadena, California. The friends' apartment building is in Pasadena, close to Caltech.

Q17. Which spin-off series follows Sheldon as a child?

Answer: Young Sheldon. Young Sheldon started in 2017 with Iain Armitage playing the young genius.

Q18. What does Sheldon call his grandmother?

Answer: Meemaw. Meemaw is Sheldon's outspoken Texan grandmother.

Q19. What is the name of Raj's little dog?

Answer: Cinnamon. Raj adopts Cinnamon, and he treats the dog like his child.

Q20. Where does Howard travel to in space during the series?

Answer: The International Space Station. Howard flies to the ISS on a Russian Soyuz spacecraft, and the trip is a big source of stress for him.

About the The Big Bang Theory quiz

The Big Bang Theory ran for twelve seasons, long enough for its characters, catchphrases, and running gags to become deeply familiar — which is why its trivia rewards the regulars. Everyone remembers Sheldon's spot and "Bazinga," so a real quiz goes further: the jobs, the relationships, the nerdy references, and the small callbacks that stack up over the years. Casual viewers know the broad strokes; devoted fans can name a character's field of research, trace the relationships, and recall the recurring jokes that defined the show. A strong quiz here sits between the famous lines and the finer details.

Questions cover all twelve seasons — Sheldon's quirks and "Bazinga," the friends' science careers, the comic-book and pop-culture references, Penny's arc, and the relationships that grow over time.
