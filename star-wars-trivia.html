Star Wars Trivia Quiz

Star Wars unfolds in a galaxy far, far away where the mystical Force separates the Jedi from the Sith. The original trilogy follows farm boy Luke Skywalker, Princess Leia, and smuggler Han Solo against the evil Galactic Empire and the masked Darth Vader, while the prequels and sequels widen the Skywalker saga across generations.

Q1. In which film does Darth Vader reveal "I am your father"?

Answer: The Empire Strikes Back. Vader tells Luke the truth during their duel on Cloud City.

Q2. Which crime lord does Han Solo owe money to in A New Hope?

Answer: Jabba the Hutt. Han owes Jabba a large debt, and Greedo tries to collect it in the cantina.

Q3. What colour is Luke's lightsaber in Return of the Jedi?

Answer: Green. Luke builds a new green lightsaber after losing his father's blue one.

Q4. Which protocol droid is fluent in over six million forms of communication?

Answer: C-3PO. C-3PO is a golden droid who is fussy, anxious and very polite.

Q5. Which Jedi Master trains Luke on the swamp planet Dagobah?

Answer: Yoda. Yoda hides on Dagobah and teaches Luke the ways of the Force.

Q6. What planet is Luke Skywalker raised on?

Answer: Tatooine. Luke grows up on a moisture farm on the desert planet Tatooine.

Q7. What is the name of Princess Leia's adoptive father?

Answer: Bail Organa. Bail Organa, a Senator from Alderaan, raises Leia as his daughter.

Q8. What are the white-armoured soldiers of the Galactic Empire called?

Answer: Stormtroopers. Stormtroopers are the Empire's infantry, known for terrible aim.

Q9. Which bounty hunter captures Han Solo for Jabba the Hutt in The Empire Strikes Back?

Answer: Boba Fett. Boba Fett delivers the frozen Han Solo to Jabba the Hutt.

Q10. What is the name of the forest moon where the Ewoks live?

Answer: Endor. The Ewoks help the Rebel Alliance to defeat the Empire on Endor.

Q11. Who is Anakin Skywalker's Padawan in The Clone Wars?

Answer: Ahsoka Tano. Ahsoka, a young Togruta, becomes Anakin's apprentice during the Clone Wars.

Q12. What is Emperor Palpatine's Sith name?

Answer: Darth Sidious. Palpatine hides as Darth Sidious while he rises to power as a Senator.

Q13. Which Jedi Master is killed by Darth Maul in The Phantom Menace?

Answer: Qui-Gon Jinn. Qui-Gon is struck down by Maul, and Obi-Wan then defeats the Sith apprentice.

Q14. Who is the genetic template for the clone army?

Answer: Jango Fett. Jango Fett, a bounty hunter, agreed to be the model for the Republic's clone troopers.

Q15. Which icy planet is home to the Rebel base in The Empire Strikes Back?

Answer: Hoth. The Imperial forces attack the Rebel base on Hoth in a huge battle with walkers.

Q16. What is Kylo Ren's birth name?

Answer: Ben Solo. Kylo Ren is the son of Han Solo and Leia Organa and was named after Obi-Wan Kenobi.

Q17. Which planet is Padmé Amidala's home?

Answer: Naboo. Padmé is a Queen and later Senator of Naboo.

Q18. What colour is Mace Windu's lightsaber?

Answer: Purple. Samuel L. Jackson requested a purple lightsaber, which makes Mace's weapon unique.

Q19. In how many parsecs does Han Solo claim the Falcon made the Kessel Run?

Answer: Less than twelve. Han's boast is a famous one, since a parsec is really a measure of distance, not time.

Q20. Who directed the original 1977 Star Wars film?

Answer: George Lucas. George Lucas wrote and directed the original film, later called A New Hope.

About the Star Wars quiz

Star Wars is one of the largest fictional universes ever built, and that scale is exactly why it makes such enduring trivia. Films, series, droids, ships, and a galaxy of characters give quiz-writers endless material, and fans love the challenge of proving how deep their knowledge runs. Casual viewers remember the big twists, the lightsabers, and the Force; the devoted can name a ship by its silhouette, trace a family tree, and place events in the right era. A strong Star Wars quiz balances the moments that defined pop culture with the details only a galaxy-far-away obsessive would know.

Questions span the films and series — the Jedi and the Sith, the Skywalker family, droids like R2-D2 and C-3PO, ships like the Millennium Falcon, and the war between the Rebellion and the Empire.

What this Star Wars quiz covers

This quiz reaches across the Star Wars saga — the central characters and their arcs, the Jedi and the Sith, the ships and droids, the key planets, and the events that shape the galaxy. Expect a spread from the iconic, widely known moments to deeper franchise detail. The early questions cover what most fans remember; the tougher ones reward people who know the universe beyond the main films.
