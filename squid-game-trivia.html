Squid Game Trivia Quiz

Squid Game follows hundreds of desperate, debt-ridden contestants who are lured into a secret competition. They play deadly versions of innocent children's games — starting with Red Light, Green Light — for an enormous cash prize, all watched over by pink-suited masked guards in a remote facility.

Q1. What is the first game the contestants play in Squid Game?

Answer: Red Light, Green Light. The first game uses a giant doll, and the players must stay still when the doll turns around.

Q2. In which country is the story of Squid Game set?

Answer: South Korea. The series is a South Korean drama about debt-ridden people invited to compete for money.

Q3. What is the name of the main character, Player 456?

Answer: Seong Gi-hun. Gi-hun is a divorced chauffeur with gambling debts who enters the games.

Q4. How many contestants take part in the games at the start?

Answer: 456. There are 456 players, and this number is echoed in Gi-hun's player number.

Q5. How much prize money is at stake in the games?

Answer: 45.6 billion won. Each player adds to the prize pool, and it makes a total of 45.6 billion won.

Q6. Which game involves carving a shape out of honeycomb candy?

Answer: Dalgona. The candy game is based on a real Korean street snack called dalgona.

Q7. Which player is the North Korean defector who is a pickpocket?

Answer: Kang Sae-byeok. Sae-byeok, Player 067, joins the games to help her family.

Q8. Which elderly player is Player 001?

Answer: Oh Il-nam. Il-nam is an older man who forms a bond with Gi-hun during the games.

Q9. What is the Front Man's real name?

Answer: Hwang In-ho. In-ho is the masked leader of the games and the brother of Jun-ho.

Q10. Who is Gi-hun's childhood friend and neighbour who enters the games?

Answer: Cho Sang-woo. Sang-woo is Player 218, and once was a top student.

Q11. Which police officer secretly enters the games to find his missing brother?

Answer: Hwang Jun-ho. Jun-ho poses as a guard as he searches for his brother.

Q12. What is the name of the final game?

Answer: Squid Game. The final game is a children's game played on a drawing shaped like a squid.

Q13. What is Player 199, Ali Abdul's, nationality?

Answer: Pakistani. Ali is a migrant worker whose kindness makes him a fan favourite.

Q14. In which game do players cross a bridge made of glass panels?

Answer: Glass Bridge. Players must choose which panel is strong enough to support their weight.

Q15. Which actor plays Seong Gi-hun?

Answer: Lee Jung-jae. Lee Jung-jae won an Emmy for his role as Gi-hun.

Q16. Who created and directed Squid Game?

Answer: Hwang Dong-hyuk. Hwang Dong-hyuk wrote the story about ten years before it was made.

Q17. In which year was the first season of Squid Game released?

Answer: 2021. The show was released on Netflix in September 2021 and became a global hit.

Q18. What colour are the tracksuits worn by the players?

Answer: Green. The players wear matching green tracksuits with a number on the chest.

Q19. What colour are the jumpsuits of the masked guards?

Answer: Pink. The guards wear pink jumpsuits with black masks.

Q20. Which three shapes appear on the guards' masks?

Answer: Circle, triangle and square. The shapes show the rank of the guards, and are a nod to the Squid Game board.

About the Squid Game quiz

Squid Game became a global phenomenon almost overnight, and its stark, simple imagery — the tracksuits, the masks, the childhood games turned deadly — is exactly what makes it so quizzable. The show is tightly contained, so the details matter: the order of the games, the numbers, the rules, and the players' backstories. Casual viewers remember the big games and the twist; devoted fans can name the players by number, recall the rules of each round, and trace the alliances that formed and broke. A good quiz here rewards close attention to a story built on precise, deliberate detail.

Questions cover the deadly contest — the players and their numbers, the games from Red Light, Green Light to the final round, the masked guards and the Front Man, and the show's biggest twists.

What this Squid Game quiz covers
