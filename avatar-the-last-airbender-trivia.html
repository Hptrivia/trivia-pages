Avatar: The Last Airbender Trivia Quiz

Avatar: The Last Airbender is set in a world split into four nations — Water, Earth, Fire, and Air — where certain people can "bend" their element. After the Fire Nation launches a hundred-year war, a young Airbender named Aang wakes from a century in the ice as the Avatar, the only one able to master all four elements, and sets out with Katara and Sokka to restore balance.

Q1. Who is the Avatar at the start of the series?

Answer: Aang. Aang is a twelve-year-old Airbender who is the last surviving Airbender and the Avatar.

Q2. Which element does Katara bend?

Answer: Water. Katara is a waterbender from the Southern Water Tribe.

Q3. What kind of animal is Aang's companion Momo?

Answer: A winged lemur. Momo is a winged lemur who joins Aang after they find him at an abandoned Air Temple.

Q4. Which nation does Prince Zuko come from?

Answer: The Fire Nation. Zuko is the banished prince of the Fire Nation, hunting the Avatar to regain his honor.

Q5. Which weapon is Sokka famous for throwing?

Answer: A boomerang. Sokka uses a boomerang, and later a sword made from a meteorite.

Q6. Who is Zuko's uncle and mentor?

Answer: Iroh. Iroh is a retired Fire Nation general who cares for Zuko and loves tea.

Q7. What is the name of the walled capital of the Earth Kingdom?

Answer: Ba Sing Se. Ba Sing Se is a giant walled city, and its secret police keep its citizens in the dark about the war.

Q8. Who becomes Aang's earthbending teacher?

Answer: Toph Beifong. Toph is a blind earthbender who calls herself the greatest earthbender in the world.

Q9. What is the name of Katara and Sokka's grandmother?

Answer: Kanna. Kanna, called Gran Gran, raised Katara and Sokka in the Southern Water Tribe.

Q10. What animal is Bosco, the Earth King's pet?

Answer: A bear. Bosco is a bear who lives in the palace at Ba Sing Se.

Q11. What is the name of Fire Lord Ozai's daughter?

Answer: Azula. Azula is a firebending prodigy who serves as Zuko's ruthless younger sister.

Q12. Which spirit takes the form of a koi fish and represents the moon?

Answer: Tui. Tui and La, the moon and ocean spirits, live as koi in the Spirit Oasis at the North Pole.

Q13. Who was the Avatar before Aang?

Answer: Roku. Roku was a Fire Nation Avatar and teacher of Aang in the Avatar state.

Q14. What is the name of the secret police force of Ba Sing Se?

Answer: The Dai Li. The Dai Li are earthbenders who protect the city's culture and keep its secrets.

Q15. What material does Toph discover she can bend?

Answer: Metal. Toph realises metal contains earth and uses it to become the first metalbender.

Q16. Who is the masked hero known as the Blue Spirit?

Answer: Zuko. Zuko wears the Blue Spirit's mask to rescue Aang from Zhao's prison.

Q17. What does Sokka call the sword he makes from a meteorite?

Answer: The space sword. Sokka's dream of a sword made from a meteorite gives him a weapon much better than a boomerang.

Q18. Which Avatar founded the Kyoshi Warriors?

Answer: Kyoshi. Avatar Kyoshi trained the Kyoshi Warriors on Kyoshi Island, and they wear distinctive green outfits.

Q19. Which Fire Nation commander tries to kill the Moon Spirit at the North Pole?

Answer: Zhao. Zhao kills the Moon Spirit, which forces Princess Yue to sacrifice herself.

Q20. For how many years was Aang frozen in the iceberg?

Answer: One hundred. Aang and Appa spent a century in the iceberg, during which the Fire Nation waged its war on the world.

About the Avatar: The Last Airbender quiz

Avatar: The Last Airbender packs an enormous amount of world-building into three seasons, which is why it holds up so well as a trivia subject. The four nations, the bending disciplines, the spirit world, and a hundred-year war all get room to breathe, and the show trusts its audience to keep track. Casual viewers remember Aang, Zuko's scar, and Appa; devoted fans can trace the exact turning points of Zuko's redemption arc, name the members of Team Avatar, and explain how each bending style mirrors a real martial art. That depth is what separates a good guess from a confident answer here.

Questions span all three books — covering Aang, Katara, Sokka, Toph, and Zuko, the four bending disciplines, the spirit world, Appa and Momo, and the war against Fire Lord Ozai.

What this Avatar: The Last Airbender quiz covers

This quiz spans all three books — Water, Earth, and Fire. Expect questions on the core group of Aang, Katara, Sokka, Toph, and Zuko, the four bending disciplines and how they work, the spirit world, companions like Appa and Momo, and the war against Fire Lord Ozai. The easier questions cover the characters and moments almost every viewer remembers; the tougher ones reward people who have watched the full series more than once.

Avatar: The Last Airbender quiz — frequently asked questions

Does this cover the sequel series too?

No — this quiz focuses on the original Avatar: The Last Airbender and its three books. Events from the follow-up series are not needed to answer anything here.

How much of the show do I need to have seen?

Ideally the whole series, since the questions draw from all three seasons. If you have only seen the first book, you will still recognise the early questions but may find the later ones harder.

Is it more about characters or the world?

Both. Some questions test your memory of the characters and their arcs, while others focus on the bending system, the nations, and the lore. A rounded knowledge of the show serves you best.
