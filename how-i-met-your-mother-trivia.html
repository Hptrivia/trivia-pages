How I Met Your Mother Trivia Quiz

How I Met Your Mother is told by Ted Mosby years in the future as he recounts to his kids the long, winding story of how he met their mother. It follows Ted and his New York friends — engaged couple Marshall and Lily, news anchor Robin, and the suit-obsessed, scheming Barney — who practically live at the bar MacLaren's.

Q1. Which actor voices Future Ted, the narrator?

Answer: Bob Saget. Bob Saget's voice tells the story to Ted's kids in 2030.

Q2. What is the name of the bar where the friends hang out?

Answer: MacLaren's. MacLaren's Pub is a New York bar that becomes the group's second home.

Q3. What is Barney Stinson's famous catchphrase?

Answer: "Legen—wait for it—dary!". Barney turns "legendary" into a joke by leaving a pause before "dary".

Q4. What is Ted Mosby's profession?

Answer: Architect. Ted starts as an architect, and later he becomes a college professor.

Q5. Which country is Robin Scherbatsky from?

Answer: Canada. Robin comes from Canada, and there are jokes about her pop star past.

Q6. What is the name of the mother of Ted's children?

Answer: Tracy McConnell. Tracy, nicknamed "The Mother", appears in person in the final season, and is the show's big reveal.

Q7. What is Marshall Eriksen's profession?

Answer: Lawyer. Marshall wants to be an environmental lawyer but takes a corporate job to pay bills.

Q8. What is Lily Aldrin's job at the start of the series?

Answer: Kindergarten teacher. Lily teaches kindergarten and paints, and later works as an art consultant.

Q9. What does Ted steal to impress Robin on their first date?

Answer: A blue French horn. The blue French horn is a running symbol of Ted's feelings for Robin.

Q10. What is the name of Robin's Canadian pop star alter ego?

Answer: Robin Sparkles. Robin Sparkles' song "Let's Go to the Mall" is a hit.

Q11. What is Barney's job, as summed up in his own acronym?

Answer: PLEASE. Barney says his job is PLEASE, an acronym that no one can explain.

Q12. What is the name of the bet where Marshall can slap Barney?

Answer: The Slap Bet. Marshall wins the right to slap Barney, and he uses the slaps very sparingly.

Q13. Which actor plays Tracy McConnell, the Mother?

Answer: Cristin Milioti. Cristin Milioti joined the cast for the final season.

Q14. What is the name of Barney's book of seduction schemes?

Answer: The Playbook. Barney has a set of elaborate schemes recorded in the Playbook.

Q15. What is the name of Marshall and Lily's son?

Answer: Marvin. Marvin Waitforit Eriksen is named in a joke on Barney.

Q16. In which year did How I Met Your Mother first air?

Answer: 2005. The show premiered on CBS in September 2005 and ran for nine seasons.

Q17. Who does Ted end up with in the final scene of the series?

Answer: Robin. In the last scene, Ted arrives at Robin's window with the blue French horn.

Q18. What is the name of Barney's brother?

Answer: James. James Stinson is Barney's older brother, and he is in a happy marriage.

Q19. Which actor plays Barney Stinson?

Answer: Neil Patrick Harris. Neil Patrick Harris played Barney, and earned multiple Emmy nominations for it.

Q20. Which actor plays Marshall Eriksen?

Answer: Jason Segel. Jason Segel plays Marshall, the big-hearted friend from Minnesota.

About the How I Met Your Mother quiz

How I Met Your Mother built its whole structure on payoffs — a story told in flashback, full of running jokes, callbacks, and mysteries that stretch across seasons. That design is a gift for trivia, because the show practically dares you to remember the details. Casual viewers know the gang and the yellow umbrella; devoted fans can explain the Bro Code, track Barney's schemes, and recall the small setups that pay off years later. A good quiz here rewards the viewers who treated the series like the puzzle it was built to be.

Questions cover all nine seasons — Ted's search for "the one," Barney's playbook and suits, Marshall and Lily, Robin, the bar MacLaren's, and catchphrases like "Legen — wait for it — dary."

What this How I Met Your Mother quiz covers

This quiz spans the series — the five friends, the bar they call home, the long-running jokes and catchphrases, the relationships, and the mysteries that drive the framing story. Expect a mix from the widely known moments to the deeper callbacks. The early questions are friendly for any fan; the later ones reward the people who followed every setup and payoff.
