The Walking Dead Trivia Quiz

The Walking Dead opens with sheriff's deputy Rick Grimes waking from a coma into a world overrun by "walkers." He sets out to find his family and becomes the reluctant leader of a band of survivors who fight not just the dead, but other desperate groups of the living.

Q1. Which character wakes up from a coma in a hospital at the start?

Answer: Rick Grimes. Rick wakes up to find the world overrun by walkers.

Q2. What is the name of Rick's wife at the start of the series?

Answer: Lori. Lori Grimes is Rick's wife and the mother of Carl, and she survives the outbreak with him.

Q3. Which character is known for using a crossbow?

Answer: Daryl Dixon. Daryl is a skilled hunter and tracker who has a crossbow.

Q4. What are zombies called in The Walking Dead?

Answer: Walkers. The characters never use the word zombie, and they refer to them as walkers.

Q5. In which US state does much of the story take place?

Answer: Georgia. The show is set in Georgia, and it was filmed there too.

Q6. Who is Rick's best friend and partner at the start of the series?

Answer: Shane Walsh. Shane is a fellow police officer who protects Rick's family and struggles with his choices.

Q7. Who is the leader of the Saviors?

Answer: Negan. Negan is a brutal but charming leader who rules by fear.

Q8. What is the name of Negan's barbed-wire baseball bat?

Answer: Lucille. Negan named his bat after his late wife.

Q9. Which woman fights with a katana?

Answer: Michonne. Michonne is a fierce and skilful fighter who comes to the group with two walkers on chains.

Q10. Which town is ruled by the Governor?

Answer: Woodbury. Woodbury seems peaceful, but its leader has a dark secret.

Q11. What is the name of the prison where the group settles?

Answer: West Georgia Correctional Facility. The group makes the prison a safe place to live for a time.

Q12. What was Glenn Rhee's job before the outbreak?

Answer: Pizza delivery driver. Glenn was a quick-thinking pizza delivery driver, and he is very good at finding supplies.

Q13. Which walled community becomes a home for the group in later seasons?

Answer: Alexandria. Alexandria is a walled town in Virginia with a leader named Deanna Monroe.

Q14. Who is Alpha's second-in-command in the Whisperers?

Answer: Beta. Beta is a huge, silent man who wears a mask made of walker skin and follows Alpha's orders.

Q15. Which actor plays Negan?

Answer: Jeffrey Dean Morgan. Jeffrey Dean Morgan joined in season six, and his intro is one of the most talked-about.

Q16. Who created The Walking Dead comic series?

Answer: Robert Kirkman. Robert Kirkman began the comic in 2003, with artist Tony Moore.

Q17. Which network airs The Walking Dead?

Answer: AMC. AMC broadcast the series from 2010 until 2022.

Q18. In which year did The Walking Dead premiere?

Answer: 2010. The show premiered on Halloween night in 2010.

Q19. What promise is written on the signs that direct survivors to Terminus?

Answer: Sanctuary for all. The signs claim that anyone who arrives will survive, but Terminus is a trap.

Q20. Which actor plays Rick Grimes?

Answer: Andrew Lincoln. Andrew Lincoln played Rick for the first nine seasons.

About the The Walking Dead quiz

The Walking Dead ran long enough to build a huge cast and a sprawling history of communities, leaders, and losses — which is exactly why its trivia goes so deep. The appeal is survival drama, and fans track the alliances and betrayals as closely as the characters do. Casual viewers remember the core survivors and the biggest villains; devoted fans can name the communities, trace who led when, and recall the deaths that defined each season. A good quiz here rewards the viewers who stuck with the group through every turn.

Questions cover the survivors — Rick's group, communities like Alexandria and the Hilltop, villains like the Governor and Negan, and the long, brutal fight to stay alive.

What this The Walking Dead quiz covers

This quiz draws from the series — the core survivors, the communities and their leaders, the major villains, the alliances and betrayals, and the moments that shaped each season. Expect a spread from the widely known events to the finer detail. The early questions cover what most viewers remember; the harder ones reward long-time watchers.
