Demon Slayer Trivia Quiz

Demon Slayer follows Tanjiro Kamado, a kind-hearted boy who comes home to find his family slaughtered by a demon and his sister Nezuko turned into one. He joins the Demon Slayer Corps to find a way to make her human again and to hunt down the demon lord responsible, Muzan Kibutsuji.

Q1. Who is the main character of Demon Slayer?

Answer: Tanjiro Kamado. Tanjiro is a kind boy who becomes a demon slayer after his family is attacked.

Q2. What is the name of Tanjiro's sister who is turned into a demon?

Answer: Nezuko. Nezuko keeps her human heart and is protected by Tanjiro throughout the story.

Q3. What does Nezuko wear in her mouth?

Answer: A bamboo tube. The bamboo muzzle prevents her from biting, and it is a symbol of her self-control.

Q4. Who is the main villain, the first and strongest demon?

Answer: Muzan Kibutsuji. Muzan is the first demon and the source of all other demons.

Q5. What is the name of the group that Tanjiro joins to fight demons?

Answer: The Demon Slayer Corps. The Demon Slayer Corps is a secret organisation which has protected humanity for centuries.

Q6. Which breathing style does Tanjiro first learn from Sakonji Urokodaki?

Answer: Water Breathing. Tanjiro trains for two years with Urokodaki to master Water Breathing.

Q7. Which character wears a boar's head?

Answer: Inosuke Hashibira. Inosuke was raised by boars and is known for his aggressive personality.

Q8. Which Hashira tells Tanjiro to "set your heart ablaze"?

Answer: Kyojuro Rengoku. Rengoku is a cheerful and strong Hashira who appears in the Mugen Train story.

Q9. Which Thunder Breathing user is a cowardly but talented swordsman who fights best when asleep?

Answer: Zenitsu Agatsuma. Zenitsu is a scared boy who becomes a master of Thunder Breathing.

Q10. Which film has Tanjiro and his friends board a train that is home to a demon?

Answer: Mugen Train. Demon Slayer: Mugen Train became one of the highest-grossing anime films.

Q11. Which Hashira spares Nezuko at the start and sends Tanjiro to Urokodaki?

Answer: Giyu Tomioka. Giyu is a quiet swordsman who chooses to give Tanjiro and Nezuko a chance.

Q12. Who is the Insect Hashira?

Answer: Shinobu Kocho. Shinobu uses poison made from wisteria to fight demons.

Q13. What are the twelve strongest demons under Muzan called?

Answer: The Twelve Kizuki. The Kizuki are ranked, and each has a title of Upper Moon or Lower Moon.

Q14. What are the demon slayers' swords called?

Answer: Nichirin blades. Nichirin blades are made from special ore which absorbs the sun's rays and change colour depending on the user.

Q15. Which Lower Rank demon controls a family of spider-like demons on Mount Natagumo?

Answer: Rui. Rui is the Lower Moon Five, and he holds his fake family together through fear.

Q16. Who is the strongest demon, Upper Moon One?

Answer: Kokushibo. Kokushibo is the top-ranked Upper Moon and was once a demon slayer.

Q17. Which ancient dance did Tanjiro's father perform every year?

Answer: Hinokami Kagura. The dance is the source of Tanjiro's Hinokami Kagura breathing techniques.

Q18. Who created the Demon Slayer manga?

Answer: Koyoharu Gotouge. Koyoharu Gotouge wrote and drew the series for Weekly Shonen Jump.

Q19. Which studio animates the Demon Slayer anime?

Answer: ufotable. ufotable is praised for beautiful animation, particularly the fluid sword fights.

Q20. How many Hashira are there in the Demon Slayer Corps?

Answer: Nine. The nine Hashira are the strongest members of the Corps, and each masters a breathing style.

About the Demon Slayer quiz

Demon Slayer pairs stunning action with a carefully built world of breathing styles, demon ranks, and slayer hierarchies — all of which give trivia plenty to work with. The appeal is the mix of emotional story and detailed lore. Casual viewers remember Tanjiro, Nezuko, and the biggest fights; devoted fans can name the Hashira, explain the breathing techniques, and rank the demons in order. A good quiz here rewards both the heart of the story and the systems fans love to study.

Questions cover Tanjiro's journey — Nezuko, the elite swordsmen called the Hashira, the breathing techniques, the Twelve Kizuki demons, and the search for Muzan.

Expect a spread from the widely loved moments to the deeper lore. The early questions cover what most viewers remember; the harder ones reward people who followed the series closely.
