The Office Trivia Quiz

The Office is a mockumentary about the staff of the Scranton branch of paper company Dunder Mifflin, led by well-meaning but excruciating regional manager Michael Scott. Across nine seasons it follows office pranks, the slow-burn romance between Jim and Pam, and oddball employees like the beet-farming, rule-obsessed Dwight Schrute.

Q1. Which paper company do the main characters of The Office work for?

Answer: Dunder Mifflin. Dunder Mifflin is a paper supplier with a branch in Scranton.

Q2. In which Pennsylvania city is the main branch of The Office set?

Answer: Scranton. The show is set in Scranton, which was also chosen for its ordinary charm.

Q3. Who is the regional manager of the Scranton branch?

Answer: Michael Scott. Michael Scott, played by Steve Carell, is an eager manager who wants to be loved.

Q4. What is the name of the beet farm owned by Dwight's family?

Answer: Schrute Farms. Dwight runs Schrute Farms as a bed and breakfast.

Q5. Which colleague does Jim Halpert have a crush on at the start of the series?

Answer: Pam Beesly. Jim is in love with Pam, the receptionist, and the two later marry.

Q6. What does the mug on Michael Scott's desk say?

Answer: World's Best Boss. Michael buys the mug for himself after a tough day.

Q7. What is the name of the company Michael starts after leaving Dunder Mifflin?

Answer: The Michael Scott Paper Company. Michael starts a rival firm with Pam and Ryan, and it forces Dunder Mifflin to buy it out.

Q8. Which accountant at Dunder Mifflin loves cats?

Answer: Angela Martin. Angela is the uptight head of the accounting team and her cats are a constant source of jokes.

Q9. Who is the HR representative that Michael dislikes?

Answer: Toby Flenderson. Toby works in HR, and Michael takes every chance to insult him.

Q10. Which line is Michael Scott best known for saying?

Answer: "That's what she said". Michael uses the joke often, and it became one of the most famous lines from the show.

Q11. What is the name of Kevin Malone's band?

Answer: Scrantonicity. Kevin plays drums in Scrantonicity, a band that only covers songs from The Police.

Q12. Which employee works in quality assurance?

Answer: Creed Bratton. Creed is the strange, mysterious quality assurance rep.

Q13. Which university did Andy Bernard attend?

Answer: Cornell. Andy mentions Cornell constantly, and he even sings songs from its glee club.

Q14. What does Jim famously put Dwight's stapler in?

Answer: Jell-O. Jim's Jell-O prank is one of the most popular pranks in the series.

Q15. Who is Michael Scott's boss at the corporate office early in the series?

Answer: Jan Levinson. Jan is the vice president who manages Michael, and later becomes involved with him.

Q16. To which Dunder Mifflin branch does Jim transfer in season two?

Answer: Stamford. Jim moves to Stamford after Pam turns down his confession, and returns after the branches merge.

Q17. Which actor plays Dwight Schrute?

Answer: Rainn Wilson. Rainn Wilson plays Dwight, the intense assistant to the regional manager, for all nine seasons.

Q18. What is the name of Dwight's cousin who works on the beet farm?

Answer: Mose. Mose is a quiet farmhand who has a few strange habits.

Q19. Which actor plays Michael Scott?

Answer: Steve Carell. Steve Carell played Michael from 2005 to 2011.

Q20. What is Ryan Howard's job when he first joins Dunder Mifflin?

Answer: Temp. Ryan starts as a temp and rises to a corporate role before a very public fall.

About the The Office quiz

The Office is one of the most rewatched comedies of its era, and that constant rewatching is exactly why its trivia has to dig deep. Everyone can quote Michael Scott and remember Jim and Pam, so a real test goes further — the cold opens, the pranks, the side characters, and the running gags that only regulars catch. Casual viewers know the big moments; superfans can name a character's job title, recall a specific prank, and place an episode from a single quote. A strong Office quiz lives in that layer of detail that turns a comfort show into a shared language.

Questions cover all nine seasons — Michael Scott's antics, the Jim and Pam romance, Dwight and Schrute Farms, the pranks, the parties, and the documentary-style moments that made the show.

What this The Office quiz covers

This quiz spans the run of Dunder Mifflin's Scranton branch — the core characters, the workplace relationships, the pranks and cold opens, the memorable one-liners, and the small recurring jokes. Expect a mix from the moments everyone quotes to the details only frequent rewatchers remember. The early questions are friendly for any fan; the later ones reward the people who have seen every episode.

Do I need to know the side characters?
