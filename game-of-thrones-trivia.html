Game of Thrones Trivia Quiz

Game of Thrones is set in the Seven Kingdoms of Westeros, where noble houses — Stark, Lannister, Targaryen, Baratheon, and more — scheme and wage war for the Iron Throne. Meanwhile an ancient threat stirs beyond the Wall in the frozen north, and across the sea Daenerys Targaryen raises three dragons and builds an army to take back her family's crown.

Q1. What colour are the eyes of Jon Snow's albino direwolf?

Answer: Red. Ghost, with white fur and red eyes, is the only direwolf that is albino.

Q2. Which noble house has a lion as its sigil?

Answer: House Lannister. The golden lion of Lannister is the sigil of the family from Casterly Rock.

Q3. Who sits on the Iron Throne when the series begins?

Answer: Robert Baratheon. King Robert took the throne after leading the rebellion against the Mad King.

Q4. What is Daenerys Targaryen's famous title?

Answer: Mother of Dragons. Daenerys hatches three dragon eggs, which earns her the title.

Q5. What is the motto of House Stark?

Answer: "Winter Is Coming". The words warn of the long winter and are the Stark family's motto.

Q6. Which Lannister is known as "the Imp"?

Answer: Tyrion. Tyrion is short, sharp and clever, and he is despised by his father.

Q7. What is the name of the Night's Watch's main castle?

Answer: Castle Black. Castle Black stands along the Wall, where the Night's Watch guards the north.

Q8. Which fighting style does Syrio Forel teach Arya Stark?

Answer: Water dancing. Syrio, a Braavosi sword master, teaches Arya to be quick and to watch closely.

Q9. How many dragon eggs does Daenerys receive as a wedding gift?

Answer: Three. The three eggs were thought to be petrified until they hatched during Khal Drogo's funeral.

Q10. What is the capital city of the Seven Kingdoms?

Answer: King's Landing. King's Landing is the seat of the Iron Throne on the east coast of Westeros.

Q11. Which house has a golden kraken on a black field as its sigil?

Answer: House Greyjoy. The Greyjoys of the Iron Islands are known for the words "We Do Not Sow".

Q12. What is the name of Jon Snow's Valyrian steel sword?

Answer: Longclaw. Jeor Mormont gives Longclaw to Jon, and the sword has a wolf's head pommel.

Q13. Which character is known as "the Kingslayer"?

Answer: Jaime Lannister. Jaime earned the name for killing King Aerys II, the Mad King.

Q14. Which Free City is home to the Faceless Men?

Answer: Braavos. Arya travels to Braavos to train with the House of Black and White.

Q15. Which character serves as the Master of Whisperers?

Answer: Varys. Varys is a eunuch and spymaster who keeps a network of little birds.

Q16. What are the names of Daenerys's three dragons?

Answer: Drogon, Rhaegal and Viserion. Daenerys names her dragons after her husband and her brothers.

Q17. What is Hodor's real name?

Answer: Wylis. Hodor is really named Wylis, though "Hodor" is nearly the only thing he says.

Q18. Which battle sees Jon Snow face Ramsay Bolton?

Answer: The Battle of the Bastards. Jon fights Ramsay outside Winterfell in the ninth episode of season six.

Q19. What is the title of the first novel in George R.R. Martin's series?

Answer: A Game of Thrones. A Game of Thrones was published in 1996 and is the book the first season adapts.

Q20. What is House Lannister's official motto?

Answer: "Hear Me Roar!". Lannisters are known for paying debts, but "Hear Me Roar!" is their true motto.

About the Game of Thrones quiz

Game of Thrones built a world dense enough to fill an encyclopedia, and that scale is what makes it such fertile ground for trivia. Great houses, sprawling family trees, shifting alliances, and a body count to match — keeping it all straight is half the fun. Casual viewers remember the dragons, the Iron Throne, and the biggest shocks; devoted fans can recite house words, trace who betrayed whom, and name the players in battles that happened seasons apart. A good quiz here rewards the viewers who treated Westeros like a place worth memorising.

Questions cover all eight seasons — the great houses and their sigils, the Iron Throne, the Night's Watch and the Wall, Daenerys and her dragons, and the major battles, betrayals, and deaths.

What this Game of Thrones quiz covers

Questions draw from across the series — the major houses of Westeros and their words, the central characters and their tangled loyalties, the key battles and betrayals, and the long fight for the Iron Throne. Expect a spread from the widely known moments to the deeper political detail. The early questions cover what most viewers remember; the harder ones reward people who kept track of the alliances, deaths, and reversals that defined each season.
