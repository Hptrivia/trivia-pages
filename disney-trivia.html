Disney Trivia Quiz

Disney's animated films stretch from early classics like Snow White, Cinderella, and The Lion King to modern blockbusters like Frozen, Moana, and Encanto. Together they have given generations a shared library of princesses, heroes, villains, talking sidekicks, and songs people can still sing by heart.

Q1. Which mouse is the mascot of the Walt Disney Company?

Answer: Mickey Mouse. Mickey Mouse debuted in 1928 in the short film Steamboat Willie.

Q2. Which princess has ice powers in Frozen?

Answer: Elsa. Elsa's powers are tied to her emotions, and she sings "Let It Go".

Q3. In which Disney film does the song "Circle of Life" appear?

Answer: The Lion King. The Circle of Life opens the film as animals gather to see the newborn Simba.

Q4. Which cowboy toy is the hero of Toy Story?

Answer: Woody. Woody is Andy's favourite toy, and he is the leader of the group.

Q5. Which Disney princess leaves behind a glass slipper?

Answer: Cinderella. Cinderella loses one glass slipper at the ball, and the prince uses it to find her.

Q6. What is the name of Jasmine's pet tiger in Aladdin?

Answer: Rajah. Rajah is Jasmine's tiger, and he is very protective of her.

Q7. Which villain curses Aurora in Sleeping Beauty?

Answer: Maleficent. Maleficent curses Aurora after not being invited to the christening.

Q8. What is the name of Ariel's fish friend in The Little Mermaid?

Answer: Flounder. Flounder is a yellow and blue fish who is always by Ariel's side.

Q9. What is the name of Kristoff's reindeer in Frozen?

Answer: Sven. Kristoff and Sven are ice harvesters who help Anna.

Q10. How many dwarfs live in the cottage in Snow White?

Answer: Seven. Snow White lives with seven dwarfs: Doc, Grumpy, Happy, Sleepy, Bashful, Sneezy and Dopey.

Q11. What is the name of the little dragon who accompanies Mulan?

Answer: Mushu. Mushu is a small, funny dragon voiced by Eddie Murphy.

Q12. What is the name of Belle's father in Beauty and the Beast?

Answer: Maurice. Maurice is an inventor who gets lost and ends up in the Beast's castle.

Q13. What is the name of Pinocchio's cricket conscience?

Answer: Jiminy Cricket. Jiminy Cricket is the wise cricket who guides Pinocchio.

Q14. Which bear befriends Mowgli in The Jungle Book?

Answer: Baloo. Baloo teaches Mowgli the carefree song "The Bare Necessities".

Q15. Which was the first fully computer-animated feature film?

Answer: Toy Story. Toy Story, made by Pixar and released in 1995, changed animation for good.

Q16. In which year was Snow White and the Seven Dwarfs released?

Answer: 1937. It was the first full-length cel-animated feature film.

Q17. Which actor voiced the Genie in the 1992 film Aladdin?

Answer: Robin Williams. Robin Williams improvised so much for the Genie that hours of recordings were made.

Q18. What is the name of Cinderella's wicked stepmother?

Answer: Lady Tremaine. Lady Tremaine treats Cinderella cruelly, and her daughters are Anastasia and Drizella.

Q19. Which Disney film features the red-haired archer princess Merida?

Answer: Brave. Merida is the princess from a Scottish kingdom who refuses an arranged marriage.

Q20. Which Disney film features the demigod Maui?

Answer: Moana. Maui is a shape-shifting demigod voiced by Dwayne Johnson.

About the Disney quiz

Disney trivia spans nearly a century of animation, characters, and songs, which is what makes it such a crowd-pleaser and such a challenge. The films are woven into childhoods around the world, so the surface knowledge is nearly universal — but the depth is enormous. Casual fans remember the princesses, the sidekicks, and the big songs; the devoted can name a voice actor, place a film by its release era, and recall the smaller characters who never got a sequel. A good Disney quiz balances the warm, familiar favourites with the detail that separates a fan from a superfan.

Questions cover the animated catalogue — classic films and modern hits, the princesses and their stories, famous villains, comic sidekicks, and the music everyone knows.

What this Disney quiz covers

This quiz reaches across the Disney animated canon — the classic and modern films, the heroes and villains, the sidekicks, the songs, and the moments that defined generations. Expect a spread from the universally known favourites to deeper details. The early questions cover the films almost everyone has seen; the harder ones reward people who know the wider catalogue.
