About the Disney quiz

Disney trivia spans nearly a century of animation, characters, and songs, which is what makes it such a crowd-pleaser and such a challenge. The films are woven into childhoods around the world, so the surface knowledge is nearly universal — but the depth is enormous. Casual fans remember the princesses, the sidekicks, and the big songs; the devoted can name a voice actor, place a film by its release era, and recall the smaller characters who never got a sequel. A good Disney quiz balances the warm, familiar favourites with the detail that separates a fan from a superfan.

Questions cover the animated catalogue — classic films and modern hits, the princesses and their stories, famous villains, comic sidekicks, and the music everyone knows.

What this Disney quiz covers

This quiz reaches across the Disney animated canon — the classic and modern films, the heroes and villains, the sidekicks, the songs, and the moments that defined generations. Expect a spread from the universally known favourites to deeper details. The early questions cover the films almost everyone has seen; the harder ones reward people who know the wider catalogue.
