Attack on Titan Trivia Quiz

Attack on Titan is set in a world where the last of humanity hides inside three enormous concentric walls to survive giant, man-eating humanoids called Titans. When a Colossal Titan smashes through the outer wall, a young man named Eren Yeager vows to join the Survey Corps, fight back, and learn the truth behind the Titans.

Q1. What does Eren Yeager vow to do to the Titans after his home is destroyed?

Answer: Kill every last one. Eren's promise to wipe out the Titans drives him to join the Survey Corps.

Q2. What are the giant humanoid creatures that threaten humanity called?

Answer: Titans. Titans are huge humanoid monsters that eat humans.

Q3. Which item does Mikasa wear that Eren gave her?

Answer: A red scarf. Eren wraps a red scarf around Mikasa, and she treasures it for years.

Q4. Which branch of the military fights Titans outside the walls?

Answer: The Survey Corps. The Survey Corps explores the outside world, at a high risk of death.

Q5. Which of Eren's best friends is an intelligent strategist?

Answer: Armin Arlert. Armin is smaller and less physically strong, but he is a genius at planning.

Q6. Who is known as humanity's strongest soldier?

Answer: Levi Ackerman. Levi is a short, sharp-tongued captain who is very good at killing Titans.

Q7. What is the name of the outermost wall?

Answer: Wall Maria. Wall Maria is breached in the first episode, which forces humanity into a smaller area.

Q8. What device allows soldiers to fly through the air?

Answer: 3D Maneuver Gear. The gear uses gas and wires to let soldiers move quickly between buildings.

Q9. Who is the commander of the Survey Corps at the start of the series?

Answer: Erwin Smith. Erwin leads by making difficult decisions, often at great human cost.

Q10. Who is the owner of the Colossal Titan?

Answer: Bertholdt Hoover. Bertholdt is a quiet soldier who is later revealed to be a warrior from Marley.

Q11. Who graduates at the top of the 104th Training Corps?

Answer: Mikasa Ackerman. Mikasa is the top graduate, and she chooses to join the Survey Corps.

Q12. Who is the Armored Titan?

Answer: Reiner Braun. Reiner is a soldier who seems reliable but who hides a huge secret.

Q13. Who is the squad leader with a great love of studying Titans?

Answer: Hange Zoë. Hange is curious and energetic, and later becomes commander of the Survey Corps.

Q14. What is the name of the island where the walls are built?

Answer: Paradis Island. The Eldians live on Paradis Island, and it is separated from the rest of the world by the ocean.

Q15. Which nation holds Eldians in internment zones in the later seasons?

Answer: Marley. Marley's leaders control the Eldian population by threatening them with the power of the Titans.

Q16. Who created the Attack on Titan manga?

Answer: Hajime Isayama. Hajime Isayama began the series in 2009 in Bessatsu Shonen Magazine.

Q17. What is the name of Eren's father?

Answer: Grisha Yeager. Grisha is a doctor who holds the secret of Titans and the basement.

Q18. Who is the owner of the Beast Titan?

Answer: Zeke Yeager. Zeke is Eren's half-brother, and he leads the Marleyan Warriors.

Q19. How many years does a Titan shifter live after gaining the power?

Answer: Thirteen. The Curse of Ymir limits shifter lifespans to thirteen years.

Q20. Which studio animated the first three seasons of Attack on Titan?

Answer: Wit Studio. Wit Studio made seasons one to three and MAPPA took over for the final season.

About the Attack on Titan quiz

Attack on Titan is built on secrets, and unravelling them is half the reason fans return to it. Every season reframes what came before, layering in new names, factions, and reveals that reward close attention. Casual viewers remember the Titans, the walls, and the main trio; devoted fans can trace the shifting alliances, name the Titan powers, and place a twist in its proper timeline. A good quiz here rewards the viewers who kept track as the story turned everything on its head.

Questions cover the war beyond the walls — Eren, Mikasa, and Captain Levi, the Survey Corps, the different Titan types, and the revelations that reshape everything fans thought they knew.

What this Attack on Titan quiz covers

This quiz spans the series — the main characters and the Survey Corps, the Titans and their powers, the walls and the world beyond them, and the reveals that redraw the story. Expect a spread from the widely known moments to the deeper detail. The early questions cover the setup most viewers remember; the harder ones reward people who followed every twist.
