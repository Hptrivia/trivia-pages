One Piece Trivia Quiz

One Piece follows Monkey D. Luffy, a cheerful young pirate whose body turned to rubber after he ate a Devil Fruit. With his Straw Hat crew, he sails the Grand Line in search of the legendary treasure called the One Piece, which would make him King of the Pirates.

Q1. Who is the main character of One Piece?

Answer: Monkey D. Luffy. Luffy is the rubber-bodied captain of the Straw Hat Pirates.

Q2. What is Luffy's dream?

Answer: To become the Pirate King. Luffy wants to find the treasure known as the One Piece and become King of the Pirates.

Q3. What fruit did Luffy eat that gave him a stretchy body?

Answer: The Gum-Gum Fruit. Luffy can stretch, but the fruit means that he cannot swim.

Q4. What symbol is on the flag of the Straw Hat Pirates?

Answer: A skull wearing a straw hat. The Straw Hats fly a Jolly Roger with a skull wearing a straw hat, based on the hat Luffy wears.

Q5. Which crew member uses three swords in battle?

Answer: Roronoa Zoro. Zoro holds a sword in each hand and one in his mouth.

Q6. Who is the navigator of the Straw Hat Pirates?

Answer: Nami. Nami is a skilled navigator and thief who loves money and oranges.

Q7. Who is the cook of the Straw Hat Pirates?

Answer: Sanji. Sanji fights with his legs to protect his hands, and he dreams of finding the All Blue.

Q8. What is the name of the Straw Hats' second ship?

Answer: Thousand Sunny. The Thousand Sunny was built by Franky, and its figurehead is a lion.

Q9. Who is the crew's doctor, a reindeer?

Answer: Tony Tony Chopper. Chopper ate the Human-Human Fruit, and he loves to receive praise.

Q10. What is the name of Luffy's sworn brother who ate the Flame-Flame Fruit?

Answer: Portgas D. Ace. Ace is the second commander of the Whitebeard Pirates and Luffy's older brother.

Q11. Who is the archaeologist of the Straw Hat Pirates?

Answer: Nico Robin. Robin is able to read ancient Poneglyphs, which are the key to the world's history.

Q12. Who is the Red-Haired Pirate who gave Luffy his straw hat?

Answer: Shanks. Shanks lent the hat to Luffy as a promise to return it when he is a great pirate.

Q13. What is the name of the dangerous sea route that pirates follow to find the One Piece?

Answer: The Grand Line. Pirates who enter the Grand Line face strange weather and powerful enemies.

Q14. Who is the sniper of the Straw Hat Pirates?

Answer: Usopp. Usopp is a brave liar who dreams of becoming a courageous warrior of the sea.

Q15. Which crew member is a cyborg shipwright known for shouting "SUPER!"?

Answer: Franky. Franky built the Thousand Sunny and loves cola, as well as big statement poses.

Q16. Which crew member is a skeleton who plays the violin?

Answer: Brook. Brook died and came back thanks to the Revive-Revive Fruit, and he is the crew's musician.

Q17. Which fish-man becomes the Straw Hats' helmsman?

Answer: Jinbe. Jinbe is a former Warlord of the Sea who joins the crew after the Whole Cake Island arc.

Q18. Who created One Piece?

Answer: Eiichiro Oda. Eiichiro Oda began the series in 1997, and it became one of the best-selling manga of all time.

Q19. In which year did the One Piece manga begin serialisation?

Answer: 1997. One Piece started in Weekly Shonen Jump in July 1997.

Q20. What is the name of Zoro's three-sword fighting style?

Answer: Santoryu. Santoryu is a style in which Zoro uses three swords at once.

About the One Piece quiz

One Piece is famous for its sheer scale — hundreds of episodes, a vast cast, and a world map crammed with islands, crews, and bounties. That immensity is what makes its trivia so beloved and so demanding. Casual viewers know Luffy, the Straw Hats, and the dream of finding the One Piece; devoted fans can recite bounties, name obscure crew members, and trace the Grand Line arc by arc. A strong quiz here rewards the long-haul fans who treated the world like a place worth mapping.

Questions cover the Straw Hat crew — Luffy, Zoro, Nami, Sanji and the rest — plus Devil Fruit powers, the seas of the Grand Line, and the many allies and enemies met along the way.

What this One Piece quiz covers

This quiz draws from Luffy's voyage — the Straw Hat crew, the powers and Devil Fruits, the islands and arcs, the rival crews and marines, and the bounties that measure a pirate's fame. Expect a spread from the widely known moments to the deeper detail. The early questions cover what most viewers remember; the harder ones reward people who have followed the long journey.
