About the Money Heist quiz

Money Heist turned an elaborate robbery into a character drama, and the intricate planning is exactly what makes it such good trivia. The code names, the plan's many moving parts, and the shifting loyalties reward viewers who paid attention. Casual watchers remember the red jumpsuits, the masks, and the Professor; devoted fans can name every member of the crew, explain the plan's key steps, and trace the relationships that complicate it. A strong quiz here rewards the people who followed the heist as closely as the police trying to stop it.

Questions cover the heists — the Professor's plans, the code-named crew, the Royal Mint and Bank of Spain, the hostages, and the tense standoffs with the police.

What this Money Heist quiz covers

This quiz draws from the series — the crew and their city code names, the Professor's plan, the heist's key stages, the hostages and negotiators, and the relationships that drive the drama. Expect a mix from the widely known moments to the finer detail. The early questions cover what most viewers remember; the harder ones reward close watchers.
