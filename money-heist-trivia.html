Money Heist Trivia Quiz

Money Heist (La Casa de Papel) follows a criminal mastermind known only as the Professor, who assembles a team that takes city code names — Tokyo, Berlin, Rio, Nairobi, Denver and more — to pull off the biggest heists in history, first on the Royal Mint of Spain and then the Bank of Spain.

Q1. What is the nickname of the mastermind behind the heist?

Answer: The Professor. The Professor plans the heist on the Royal Mint of Spain from the shadows.

Q2. What do the robbers wear during the heist?

Answer: Red jumpsuits and Salvador Dalí masks. The red jumpsuits and Dalí masks became one of the most popular costumes from a series.

Q3. Which building do the robbers take over in the first part?

Answer: The Royal Mint of Spain. The gang plan to print billions of euros in the Royal Mint.

Q4. In which language was Money Heist originally made?

Answer: Spanish. The show was made in Spain under the title La Casa de Papel.

Q5. Which robber narrates the story?

Answer: Tokyo. Tokyo tells the story from her point of view, and explains events in a voice-over.

Q6. Which hostage joins the gang and takes the name Stockholm?

Answer: Mónica Gaztambide. Mónica is taken hostage in the Mint, and she joins the robbers after falling for Denver.

Q7. Which Italian song becomes the show's anthem?

Answer: Bella Ciao. Bella Ciao is an old Italian protest song, and the robbers sing it as a symbol of resistance.

Q8. Which inspector negotiates with the robbers?

Answer: Raquel Murillo. Raquel begins as the police negotiator and later becomes part of the gang.

Q9. What do the robbers use as codenames?

Answer: City names. Members use names such as Tokyo, Berlin, Rio and Denver.

Q10. Which robber takes control of the hostages inside the Mint?

Answer: Berlin. Berlin is the cold, charming and unpredictable leader inside the Mint.

Q11. Which actress plays Tokyo?

Answer: Úrsula Corberó. Úrsula Corberó plays Silene Oliveira, whose codename is Tokyo.

Q12. Which building do the gang target in Part 3?

Answer: The Bank of Spain. The gang decide to steal the country's gold reserves.

Q13. Which member is known for his distinctive laugh?

Answer: Denver. Denver's loud laugh is used as a running joke through the series.

Q14. Who is Moscow's son?

Answer: Denver. Moscow and Denver are father and son, and they join the gang together.

Q15. Which actor plays the Professor?

Answer: Álvaro Morte. Álvaro Morte played Sergio Marquina in all five parts.

Q16. What does the Spanish title La Casa de Papel mean in English?

Answer: The House of Paper. The title refers to the Royal Mint, which is a building where money is printed on paper.

Q17. How many parts does Money Heist have?

Answer: Five. The show ran for five parts from 2017 to 2021.

Q18. In which year was the first part first shown in Spain?

Answer: 2017. The show first aired in Spain in May 2017 on Antena 3.

Q19. Which streaming service made the show a global hit?

Answer: Netflix. Netflix bought the series and released a re-edited version for the world.

Q20. Which Spanish TV channel first broadcast Money Heist?

Answer: Antena 3. The show started on Antena 3, before it was picked up by Netflix.

About the Money Heist quiz

Money Heist turned an elaborate robbery into a character drama, and the intricate planning is exactly what makes it such good trivia. The code names, the plan's many moving parts, and the shifting loyalties reward viewers who paid attention. Casual watchers remember the red jumpsuits, the masks, and the Professor; devoted fans can name every member of the crew, explain the plan's key steps, and trace the relationships that complicate it. A strong quiz here rewards the people who followed the heist as closely as the police trying to stop it.

Questions cover the heists — the Professor's plans, the code-named crew, the Royal Mint and Bank of Spain, the hostages, and the tense standoffs with the police.
